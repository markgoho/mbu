Hiking Merit Badge Requirements

01

Health & Safety

check_circle

Explain to your counselor the most likely hazards you may encounter while hiking, and what you should do to anticipate, help prevent, mitigate, and respond to these hazards.

check_circle

Show that you know first aid for injuries or illnesses that could occur while hiking, including hypothermia, frostbite, dehydration, heat exhaustion, heatstroke, sunburn, blisters, and hyperventilation.

02

Ethics & Conduct

check_circle

Explain and demonstrate the importance of the Leave No Trace Seven Principles and the Outdoor Code in hiking.

check_circle

Explain why it is important to hike with a buddy and what to do if you get lost.

03

Planning & Equipment

check_circle

Explain how to plan for a hike and the importance of having a written plan shared with someone at home.

check_circle

Discuss the appropriate clothing and footwear for hiking under various weather conditions and terrain types.

check_circle

Prepare a hiking kit with the Scout Basic Essentials plus any additional items needed for a specific hike.

04

The Hikes

Take the following five hikes of at least 10 continuous miles each, and one hike of at least 20 continuous miles.

check_circle One 10-mile hike
check_circle One 10-mile hike
check_circle One 10-mile hike
check_circle One 10-mile hike
check_circle One 10-mile hike
F
The 20-Mile Finale

One hike of at least 20 continuous miles.

05

Reporting

After each of the hikes in requirement 4, write a short report of your experience. Give dates and descriptions of routes covered, the weather, and any interesting things you saw.
