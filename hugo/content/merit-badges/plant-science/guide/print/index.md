## Plant Science Merit Badge Print Companion

Use this printable page as a quick reference while you work through the full guide. It is not meant to replace the main pages. It helps you keep track of what to observe, what to record, and what to discuss with your counselor.

## Main Topics to Review

- Parts of a flowering plant and what each part does
- Photosynthesis and why people depend on plants
- Pollination and the importance of honeybees and other insects
- Soil, water, light, temperature, and pest effects on plants
- Propagation by seed, roots, cuttings, tubers, or grafting
- Native, cultivated, and invasive plants in your area
- A career or hobby connection to plant science
- One major option track: Agronomy, Horticulture, or Field Botany
- Outdoor ethics in natural areas

## Field Notes Checklist

- Date and location
- Weather conditions
- Habitat type
- Plant names observed
- Native or non-native status when relevant
- Soil or moisture notes
- Any insects, disease signs, or weed pressure
- Questions to ask your counselor

## Option 8 Planning Notes

### Agronomy

Best if you can observe crops, grow a small plot, or visit farms and grain-related businesses.

### Horticulture

Best if you can work with gardens, containers, nurseries, or landscape plants through a season.

### Field Botany

Best if you have access to parks, preserves, Scout camps, or other natural areas for repeated observation.

## Talk With Your Counselor About

- Which Option 8 track fits your location and season
- What collecting is allowed and what should only be photographed
- How you will document projects that take place over several weeks
- Which observations they want to see in your notebook or records
