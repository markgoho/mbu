{{< drg/requirement number="8c6b2" >}}
Use the transect method to study the two different kinds of plant communities. The transects should be at least 500 feet long.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

A transect is a measured line laid across a habitat so you can sample what occurs along it in a repeatable way. The point is not to study everything everywhere. The point is to study one line carefully enough that you can compare two places fairly.

## How to Set Up a Transect

{{< drg/checklist title="Transect Basics" subtitle="Keep your method simple and repeatable" >}}
- Mark a straight line at least 500 feet long.
- Keep the line placement consistent with your study goal.
- Record the start and end points clearly.
- Use the same method at both sites.
- Decide in advance how you will record trees near the line.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

You might use a tape measure, marked rope, or mapping app. What matters most is that you can explain the setup and repeat it if needed.
