{{< drg/requirement number="8c6" >}}
Do ALL of the requirements in ONE of the following alternatives:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You must choose exactly one project. Each option teaches field botany in a different way, so pick the project that best matches your access, skill level, and time.

## Your Options

- **[Req 8c6a — Tree Inventory](/merit-badges/plant-science/guide/req8c6a/)**: Identify trees, document them with specimens, measure size, and teach others how to use a key.
- **[Req 8c6b — Transect Study](/merit-badges/plant-science/guide/req8c6b/)**: Compare two plant communities along long straight study lines and graph the results.
- **[Req 8c6c — Nested Plot Study](/merit-badges/plant-science/guide/req8c6c/)**: Use large, medium, and small plots to compare plant layers at two sites.
- **[Req 8c6d — Visiting an Herbarium](/merit-badges/plant-science/guide/req8c6d/)**: Study how plant collections are stored, organized, and used by researchers.
- **[Req 8c6e — Visiting a Plant Conservation Organization](/merit-badges/plant-science/guide/req8c6e/)**: Learn directly from people protecting rare plants and natural areas.

## How to Choose

| Option | Best if you want to... | Best if you have access to... |
|---|---|---|
| Tree Inventory | focus on trees and teaching identification | neighborhoods, parks, or camps with many trees |
| Transect Study | collect field data and make graphs | two long study areas with different plant communities |
| Nested Plot Study | compare vegetation layers in detail | sites where you can mark and measure plots |
| Herbarium Visit | learn indoor research methods | a university, botanical garden, or online herbarium |
| Conservation Visit | see real protection work in action | a nature agency or conservation group nearby |

{{< drg/tip >}}
If you enjoy careful measurement and charts, choose Transect or Nested Plot. If you prefer visits and interviews, Herbarium or Conservation Visit may fit better.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
