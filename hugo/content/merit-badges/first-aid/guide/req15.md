{{< drg/requirement number="15" >}}
Do ONE of the following:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

This requirement is your chance to see how first-aid skills connect to real people, real careers, and real service. You must choose exactly **one** option, so pick the one that gives you the clearest, most interesting look at emergency medical services.

## Your Options

- **[Req 15a — Visit an EMS Station or Training Center](/merit-badges/first-aid/guide/req15a/)**: Go in person, see equipment and spaces for yourself, and talk directly with first responders about what they do. This option is great if you learn best by being on site and asking questions face-to-face.
- **[Req 15b — Interview an EMS Professional](/merit-badges/first-aid/guide/req15b/)**: Have a focused conversation with a paramedic, EMT, nurse, dispatcher, or another EMS professional about the realities of the job. This option helps you understand daily duties, career decisions, and the human side of emergency care.
- **[Req 15c — Research an EMS Career](/merit-badges/first-aid/guide/req15c/)**: Compare career paths and dive deeply into one role's training, education, costs, and opportunities. This option builds research skills and helps you think seriously about long-term career planning.
- **[Req 15d — Apply EMS Skills to Your Life](/merit-badges/first-aid/guide/req15d/)**: Explore how emergency-care knowledge can support a hobby, volunteer role, or healthy lifestyle goal. This option is a strong fit if you want to connect first aid to outdoor adventure, coaching, lifeguarding, community service, or personal readiness.

## How to Choose

{{< drg/checklist title="Choosing Your Option" subtitle="Pick the path that gives you the best experience, not just the easiest one" >}}
- **Access**: If you already have a family connection, troop contact, or local station willing to host you, Req 15a can be very practical. If scheduling a visit is hard, Req 15b or 15c may be easier to arrange.
- **Learning style**: Choose Req 15a if you want to see spaces, vehicles, and gear. Choose Req 15b if you learn best through conversation. Choose Req 15c if you like comparing facts and building a report. Choose Req 15d if you want to connect first aid to the way you already live or want to live.
- **What you will gain**: Req 15a gives you a ground-level view of how EMS serves the community. Req 15b gives you personal insight into the people behind the job. Req 15c gives you a realistic picture of training and career pathways. Req 15d helps you turn emergency-care knowledge into a practical advantage in your own life.
- **Time and preparation**: Req 15a may require travel and permission. Req 15b may require planning thoughtful interview questions. Req 15c needs careful research and note-taking. Req 15d needs reflection, planning, and research into extra training and organizations.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

| Option | Best for Scouts who want to… | Main challenge |
|---|---|---|
| 15a | see EMS in action up close | scheduling an in-person visit |
| 15b | learn from one person's experience | asking strong questions |
| 15c | explore careers in depth | organizing research clearly |
| 15d | connect first aid to a hobby or lifestyle | thinking beyond the badge |

{{< drg/tip >}}
If you are even a little curious about a medical or rescue career, choose the option that puts you in direct contact with real professionals. Firsthand conversations usually teach you more than a stack of articles.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

Once you choose your path, start with the station or training-center visit option and see how emergency care looks from the inside.
