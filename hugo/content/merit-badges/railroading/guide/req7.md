{{< drg/requirement number="7" >}}
Do ONE of the following special-interest options:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Requirement 7 is your chance to go deep on the side of railroading that excites you most. You choose **one** of two paths — hands-on model railroading or real-world railfanning — and then complete two activities within that path.

## Your Options

### Option A — Model Railroading

Build things. Design layouts, assemble kits, create miniature scenery, and operate trains. This option is ideal if you enjoy hands-on craft projects, working at home or in a club setting, and the satisfaction of creating a miniature railroad world from scratch.

**Within Option A, you'll choose 2 of 8 activities**, ranging from drawing a layout plan to understanding digital command control systems.

[Explore the Model Railroading Option →](/merit-badges/railroading/guide/req7a/)

### Option B — Railfanning

Get out and explore. Visit museums, ride historic railroads, research preservation groups, and plan a real rail trip. This option is ideal if you enjoy history, travel, photography, and experiencing the real railroad world firsthand.

**Within Option B, you'll choose 2 of 4 activities**, each taking you outside and into genuine railroad culture.

[Explore the Railfanning Option →](/merit-badges/railroading/guide/req7b/)

---


## How to Choose

| | Model Railroading | Railfanning |
|---|---|---|
| **Where it happens** | Home, club, or workshop | Museums, depots, trains |
| **Main skills** | Building, designing, wiring | Researching, observing, documenting |
| **Best if you like** | Craft projects and scale models | History, travel, and photography |
| **Cost** | Moderate (kit materials) | Moderate (admission and tickets) |
| **Works solo?** | Yes | Yes, though a buddy or parent often joins |

{{< drg/tip >}}
Already part of a local model railroad club, or have family members who model? Option A is a natural fit. If you love train history and road trips, or there's a scenic railroad or museum nearby, Option B will feel more like an adventure than a requirement.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
