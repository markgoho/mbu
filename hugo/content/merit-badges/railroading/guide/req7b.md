{{< drg/requirement number="7" option="Railfanning" >}}
Option B—Railfanning. With your parent or guardian's and counselor's approval, do TWO of the following:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You must complete **exactly 2** of the 4 activities listed below. Railfanning is the hobby of observing, photographing, and exploring real railroads — past and present. Each activity takes you somewhere, connects you with history, or builds genuine knowledge of the railroad world.


## Your Four Activities

### 7b1 — Museum or Public Event Visit

Visit a railroad museum, historical display, or a prototype railroad–sponsored public event. Photograph, sketch, or digitally record items of interest (with permission). Then explain what you saw and describe your documentation to your counselor.

**Best if you:** Have a railroad museum, heritage railway, or railroad-sponsored event within reasonable driving distance.

[Go to Museum or Public Event Visit →](/merit-badges/railroading/guide/req7b1/)

### 7b2 — Ride a Historic Railroad

Purchase tickets and ride a scenic or historic railroad. Under supervision, photograph the equipment. Then discuss with your counselor the historic significance of the operation.

**Best if you:** Want an immersive experience — the sights, sounds, and smells of a real operating historic train.

[Go to Ride a Historic Railroad →](/merit-badges/railroading/guide/req7b2/)

### 7b3 — Rail Preservation Groups

Locate the websites of four rail historical groups. Research the history of each group's preservation operations and purpose. Talk with a member of one group and find out how you might help.

**Best if you:** Enjoy internet research and would like to explore volunteer or preservation opportunities.

[Go to Rail Preservation Groups →](/merit-badges/railroading/guide/req7b3/)

### 7b4 — Plan and Take a Rail Trip

Plan a trip by rail between two points. Obtain a schedule and identify when the train arrives at two intermediate stops. Purchase the tickets and make the trip. Explain to your counselor what you observed along the way.

**Best if you:** Want to experience modern passenger rail (Amtrak or commuter lines) and can plan a multi-stop journey.

[Go to Plan and Take a Rail Trip →](/merit-badges/railroading/guide/req7b4/)

## How to Choose Your Two

{{< drg/tip >}}
Activities 7b1 and 7b2 are natural pairs — a museum visit and a steam train ride together make for a rich, full day of railfanning. If getting out is harder, pair the online research of 7b3 with the trip planning of 7b4, which can be done on Amtrak from most cities.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
