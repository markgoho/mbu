{{< drg/requirement number="2a" >}}
Give a short biographical summary of any TWO of the following, and tell of their roles in how Scouting developed and grew in the United States.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You need to pick **two** of the nine Scouting leaders listed below. For each person you choose, you will prepare a short biographical summary and explain how they helped Scouting develop and grow in the United States.

## Your Options

- **[Req 2a1 — Daniel Carter Beard](/merit-badges/scouting-heritage/guide/req2a1/)**: Founded the Sons of Daniel Boone and became the first National Scout Commissioner. A rugged outdoorsman who shaped Scouting's wilderness identity.
- **[Req 2a2 — William D. Boyce](/merit-badges/scouting-heritage/guide/req2a2/)**: The businessman who incorporated the Boy Scouts of America in 1910 after a chance encounter with a Scout in London fog.
- **[Req 2a3 — Waite Phillips](/merit-badges/scouting-heritage/guide/req2a3/)**: An Oklahoma oil baron who donated the land that became Philmont Scout Ranch — Scouting's most famous high-adventure base.
- **[Req 2a4 — Ernest Thompson Seton](/merit-badges/scouting-heritage/guide/req2a4/)**: A naturalist and author who founded the Woodcraft Indians and served as the BSA's first Chief Scout. Brought nature lore and woodcraft traditions into the Scouting program.
- **[Req 2a5 — James E. West](/merit-badges/scouting-heritage/guide/req2a5/)**: The BSA's first Chief Scout Executive, who built the organization's professional structure and led it for 32 years.
- **[Req 2a6 — "Green Bar Bill" Hillcourt](/merit-badges/scouting-heritage/guide/req2a6/)**: The Danish-born Scouter who wrote three editions of the *Scout Handbook* and championed the patrol method for decades.
- **[Req 2a7 — Frederick Russell Burnham](/merit-badges/scouting-heritage/guide/req2a7/)**: The American-born scout and adventurer whose real-world reconnaissance skills in Africa directly inspired Baden-Powell's vision for Scouting.
- **[Req 2a8 — Dr. Charles Eastman](/merit-badges/scouting-heritage/guide/req2a8/)**: A Dakota Sioux physician and author who helped shape the BSA's early outdoor and campcraft programs, bridging Native American traditions and Scouting.
- **[Req 2a9 — Edgar Robinson](/merit-badges/scouting-heritage/guide/req2a9/)**: A YMCA leader who was instrumental in the BSA's early organization and helped bring the Scouting movement to a national audience.

## How to Choose

Pick the two people who interest you most. There is no wrong combination. Here are some ways to think about it:

- **Interested in the founding story?** Boyce, Beard, and Seton are the three men most directly involved in creating the BSA.
- **Interested in adventure and the outdoors?** Burnham, Phillips, and Beard each brought a spirit of frontier exploration to Scouting.
- **Interested in how the organization was built?** West and Robinson shaped the BSA's national structure and growth.
- **Interested in Scouting's program and traditions?** Hillcourt, Seton, and Eastman each left a lasting mark on what Scouts actually *do*.

{{< drg/tip >}}
You only need two, but reading the summaries for all nine will give you a much richer understanding of how Scouting came together. Each person contributed something different, and together their stories explain why Scouting in America looks the way it does today.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

Start with the first option, or jump directly to any person using the navigation menu.
