You must choose exactly **one** veterinary career from Req 1 or Req 2, then use that same career for all five reflection pages that follow. This requirement is not asking for the "best" career. It is asking you to think honestly about fit.

## Your Reflection Questions

- **[Req 7a — Why This Career Fits](/merit-badges/veterinary-medicine/guide/req7a/)**: Why would someone choose this field in the first place?
- **[Req 7b — What You Would Enjoy](/merit-badges/veterinary-medicine/guide/req7b/)**: What parts of the day-to-day work would appeal to you?
- **[Req 7c — The Hardest Parts of the Job](/merit-badges/veterinary-medicine/guide/req7c/)**: What challenges would make this career difficult?
- **[Req 7d — What Might Surprise You](/merit-badges/veterinary-medicine/guide/req7d/)**: What unexpected part of the job stands out most?
- **[Req 7e — Learning Never Stops](/merit-badges/veterinary-medicine/guide/req7e/)**: How does someone stay qualified and keep growing in this profession?

## How to choose your career focus

{{< drg/checklist title="Choosing One Career To Reflect On" subtitle="Pick a role you can discuss in detail" >}}
- **Pick one that feels real to you**: A field you observed in Req 6 is often easiest.
- **Choose a field with enough detail**: You need to discuss motivation, rewards, challenges, surprises, and continuing education.
- **Think beyond "I like animals"**: Good career reflection includes science, schedule, stress, teamwork, environment, and public impact.
- **Stick with the same field for all five subpages**: Your answers should build on each other.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

{{< drg/tip >}}
A strong reflection sounds specific. Instead of saying, "I would choose exotic medicine because it is cool," say, "I would choose exotic medicine because I like learning species-specific husbandry and solving unusual habitat-related medical problems."
{{< /drg/tip >}}

## What you will gain from this requirement

This requirement teaches you how to think like someone making a real career choice. You are learning to weigh purpose, interest, stress, lifestyle, and long-term growth instead of chasing a job title alone.

Begin with the first question: why would someone choose this field at all?
