{{< drg/requirement number="5" >}}
Demonstrate the use of at least THREE of the following stamp collector's tools:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You must choose exactly 3 options from this requirement. This overview page helps you pick tools that match the kinds of stamps and projects you expect to handle most often.

## Your Options

- **[Req 5a — Using Stamp Tongs](/merit-badges/stamp-collecting/guide/req5a/)**: Learn the safest everyday way to pick up stamps without bending corners or leaving skin oils on them.
- **[Req 5b — Soaking With Water and a Tray](/merit-badges/stamp-collecting/guide/req5b/)**: Remove paper from used stamps and prepare them for drying and storage.
- **[Req 5c — Inspecting With Magnifiers](/merit-badges/stamp-collecting/guide/req5c/)**: Spot fine details, flaws, and printing clues that your eyes may miss.
- **[Req 5d — Hinges and Mounts](/merit-badges/stamp-collecting/guide/req5d/)**: Compare two common methods for attaching stamps to album pages.
- **[Req 5e — Measuring With a Perforation Gauge](/merit-badges/stamp-collecting/guide/req5e/)**: Measure perforations so you can tell similar-looking issues apart.
- **[Req 5f — Safe Storage Sleeves](/merit-badges/stamp-collecting/guide/req5f/)**: Use glassines and sleeves to store loose stamps and covers without damage.
- **[Req 5g — Finding Watermarks](/merit-badges/stamp-collecting/guide/req5g/)**: Reveal hidden paper marks that can identify the exact stamp issue.

## How to Choose

{{< drg/checklist title="Choosing your three tools" subtitle="Think about what kind of collecting you plan to do most" >}}
- **Best beginner foundation**: Tongs, magnifiers, and storage sleeves are useful almost every day.
- **Best for used stamps from mail**: Water and tray, tongs, and storage sleeves help with safe removal and sorting.
- **Best for identifying tricky stamps**: Magnifiers, perforation gauge, and watermark fluid help you separate similar issues.
- **Best for building albums**: Hinges and mounts, tongs, and magnifiers help you present stamps neatly and safely.
- **What you'll gain**: Some tools help with handling, some with identification, and some with presentation. Pick a mix that shows more than one skill.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

{{< drg/tip >}}
If you are unsure, choose at least one handling tool, one identification tool, and one storage or mounting tool. That shows your counselor you understand the full workflow of collecting, not just one narrow task.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

{{< drg/safety-first >}}
Use only the tools you understand. Watermark fluid and similar products should be used with adult guidance, good ventilation, and careful handling. Keep liquids away from flames, food, and your eyes.
{{< /drg/safety-first >}}

Once you pick your three tools, start with stamp tongs. They are one of the most basic habits of careful collecting.
