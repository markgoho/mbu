{{< drg/requirement number="7" >}}
Do TWO of the following:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You must choose exactly 2 options from this requirement. These choices let you shape the badge around your interests: art, research, visiting real stamp spaces, writing, or building a display.

## Your Options

- **[Req 7a — Design Work](/merit-badges/stamp-collecting/guide/req7a/)**: Create your own stamp, cancellation, or cachet and think like a designer.
- **[Req 7b — Visit the Stamp World](/merit-badges/stamp-collecting/guide/req7b/)**: Visit a post office, stamp club, or stamp show and learn by observing real collectors and postal professionals.
- **[Req 7c — Review a Stamp Article](/merit-badges/stamp-collecting/guide/req7c/)**: Read something interesting about philately and explain what was worth learning.
- **[Req 7d — Research Stamp History](/merit-badges/stamp-collecting/guide/req7d/)**: Investigate a person or a famous stamp story and report what makes it important.
- **[Req 7e — How Stamps Are Made](/merit-badges/stamp-collecting/guide/req7e/)**: Study the production process from design through paper, printing, perforation, and gum.
- **[Req 7f — Build a Stamp Display](/merit-badges/stamp-collecting/guide/req7f/)**: Turn stamps and supporting material into a two- to three-page story exhibit.

## How to Choose

{{< drg/checklist title="Choosing your two projects" subtitle="Match the options to the kind of work you enjoy most" >}}
- **If you like art and design**: Req 7a gives you the most room for creativity.
- **If you like field trips and live events**: Req 7b puts you in real stamp-collecting spaces.
- **If you like reading and writing**: Req 7c turns research into a written review.
- **If you like history**: Req 7d helps you chase a story behind a person or a famous issue.
- **If you like process and how-things-work topics**: Req 7e is the best technical option.
- **If you like presentation and storytelling**: Req 7f lets you build something visual from your collection.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

{{< drg/tip >}}
A strong pair is one creative option and one research option. For example, 7a plus 7d or 7b plus 7f shows range and makes your counselor conversation more interesting.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

After you choose, start with the design option. Even if you do not select it, it is a good example of how collectors turn ideas into something memorable.
