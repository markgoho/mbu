{{< drg/requirement number="8" >}}
Mount and show, in a purchased or homemade album, ONE of the following:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You must choose exactly one option from this requirement. This is your final big collection project, so choose the one that best matches the stamps you can realistically gather and the story you want your album to tell.

## Your Options

- **[Req 8a — 250 Stamps From 15 Countries](/merit-badges/stamp-collecting/guide/req8a/)**: Build a broad international collection that proves you can sort and organize many different stamps.
- **[Req 8b — 50 Countries on Maps](/merit-badges/stamp-collecting/guide/req8b/)**: Combine stamps with geography by mounting one stamp from each of 50 countries in map-based pages.
- **[Req 8c — One Country or Region](/merit-badges/stamp-collecting/guide/req8c/)**: Go deeper into one nation or a connected group of countries.
- **[Req 8d — A Thematic Collection](/merit-badges/stamp-collecting/guide/req8d/)**: Build a topic-driven album such as Scouting, birds, sports, or space.
- **[Req 8e — A 30-Day Mail Study](/merit-badges/stamp-collecting/guide/req8e/)**: Observe your household mail for a month and build a collection from real postal items you discover.

## How to Choose

{{< drg/checklist title="Choosing the right final project" subtitle="Pick the option that fits your time, supply, and interests" >}}
- **Best for lots of variety**: Req 8a gives you the widest mix of countries and designs.
- **Best for geography lovers**: Req 8b turns collecting into a map project.
- **Best for focused study**: Req 8c works well if you already like one country or region.
- **Best for personal interests**: Req 8d lets you connect stamps to a favorite topic.
- **Best for everyday discovery**: Req 8e uses the mail that actually reaches your house over time.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

Choose the option you can finish neatly, not the one that only sounds impressive. A well-organized, fully explained collection is better than a huge unfinished pile.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

Once you choose, start with the broad international collection option to see how the final pages are supposed to come together.
