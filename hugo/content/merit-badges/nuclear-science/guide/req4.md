Classic Experiments. Do TWO of the following:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You must choose exactly 2 options from this requirement.

## Your Options

- **[Req 4a — Build an Electroscope](/merit-badges/nuclear-science/guide/req4a/)**: Build a simple detector that shows how electric charge behaves and how radiation can discharge it.
- **[Req 4b — See Tracks in a Cloud Chamber](/merit-badges/nuclear-science/guide/req4b/)**: Watch ionizing radiation leave visible trails in a supersaturated vapor.
- **[Req 4c — Model Half-Life](/merit-badges/nuclear-science/guide/req4c/)**: Use a hands-on experiment to show how radioactive decay follows a pattern even when individual atoms decay randomly.

## How to Choose

{{< drg/checklist title="Choosing your two experiments" subtitle="Think about materials, time, and what you want to understand" >}}
- **Best low-cost build**: 4a usually uses simple household materials and teaches electrostatics clearly.
- **Best wow-factor demonstration**: 4b lets you see radiation tracks, but it takes more setup and careful adult help.
- **Best for graphing and math-minded Scouts**: 4c turns decay into data you can count and discuss.
- **What you will gain**: 4a builds understanding of charge and detection, 4b helps you visualize invisible particles, and 4c helps you explain probability, decay, and half-life.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

{{< drg/tip >}}
A balanced pair is often 4b plus either 4a or 4c. That way one experiment helps you see radiation, and the other helps you measure or model it.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
