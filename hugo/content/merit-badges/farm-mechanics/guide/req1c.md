Find all the universal warning and safety symbols on a piece of equipment and explain what they mean.

Every tractor, combine, mower, and auger you will ever encounter is covered in warning decals and safety symbols. These are not decoration — they are a visual language designed to protect you from injury, even if you have never read the operator's manual. Understanding this language is a non-negotiable part of working around farm equipment.

## The ISO Safety Symbol System

Farm equipment manufacturers worldwide use symbols standardized by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO). These symbols communicate hazards without relying on any particular language, which means a tractor built in Germany, Japan, or the United States uses the same warning graphics.

### Signal Words and Colors

Safety labels combine a symbol with a signal word that tells you how serious the hazard is:

| Signal Word | Color | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **DANGER** | Red | Immediate hazard that **will** cause death or serious injury if not avoided |
| **WARNING** | Orange | Hazard that **could** cause death or serious injury |
| **CAUTION** | Yellow | Hazard that **could** cause minor or moderate injury |
| **NOTICE** | Blue | Important information not related to personal injury (e.g., equipment damage) |

The shape matters too. A triangle (point up) means "warning" or "caution." A circle with a diagonal line through it means "prohibited" (do not do this). A solid circle means "mandatory" (you must do this).

### Common Farm Equipment Symbols

Here are the symbols you are most likely to find on a piece of farm equipment. When you do your inspection for this requirement, look for every one of these — and any others the manufacturer has added.

**Pinch point** — Two arrows pointing toward each other between two surfaces. Found near gears, belts, rollers, and closing mechanisms. Keep hands and clothing away from these areas.

**Entanglement hazard** — A hand being pulled into a rotating shaft or gear. Found near PTO shafts, augers, fan belts, and any spinning component. This is one of the most dangerous hazards on a farm.

**Crush hazard** — A hand or body being pressed between two heavy objects. Found near hydraulic arms, loader buckets, three-point hitches, and anywhere heavy components move.

**Shear point** — Scissors-like symbol showing two blades coming together. Found on mowers, cutters, shears, and header reels. Disengage and lock out before servicing.

**Hot surface** — A hand pulling away from wavy heat lines. Found on exhaust manifolds, turbochargers, hydraulic pumps after extended use, and engine blocks.

**Electrical hazard** — A lightning bolt. Found near batteries, alternators, starter motors, and electrical panels. Disconnect the battery before working on electrical systems.

**Read the manual** — An open book symbol. Found near controls, adjustment points, and complex assemblies. It means: stop, go read the operator's manual for this specific procedure before you proceed.

**No open flame** — A flame inside a prohibition circle. Found near fuel tanks, battery compartments, and chemical storage areas.

{{< drg/safety-first >}}
If a safety decal on a piece of equipment is faded, torn, or missing, replace it before using the machine. Manufacturers sell replacement decals, and many provide them for free. A missing warning label is not just a cosmetic problem — it is a safety hazard for the next person who uses that equipment.

## How to Complete This Requirement

Choose any piece of engine-powered farm equipment — a tractor, riding mower, combine, skid steer, or even a stationary engine like a generator or irrigation pump. Then:

1. Walk around the entire machine, top to bottom, left to right.
2. Find every safety decal and warning symbol.
3. Write down or photograph each one.
4. Look up any symbol you do not recognize in the operator's manual.
5. Be prepared to explain to your counselor what each symbol means and why it is placed where it is.

{{< drg/tip >}}
Pay special attention to the PTO area at the rear of a tractor. This is one of the most dangerous zones on any farm, and it will have multiple warning decals about entanglement, shielding, and safe operating speed. If the PTO shield or guard is missing, note that as a safety problem.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
