Do ONE of the following:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You must choose exactly one of these two paths. Both strengthen your observation skills, but they ask you to show them in different ways.

## Your Options

- **[Req 4c3a — Build a 10-Specimen Collection](/merit-badges/geology/guide/req4c3a/)**: Gather and label ten rocks or minerals, record where each came from, and identify class, origin, composition, and properties. You will gain a personal reference collection.
- **[Req 4c3b — Identify 15 Specimens](/merit-badges/geology/guide/req4c3b/)**: Work with your counselor to identify fifteen rocks and minerals. You will gain more direct practice recognizing specimens accurately.

## How to Choose

{{< drg/checklist title="Collection or Identification?" subtitle="Which path fits you better?" >}}
- **Choose 4c3a if** you like organizing, labeling, and keeping a set of specimens.
- **Choose 4c3b if** you want more guided identification practice without building a permanent collection.
- **What you gain:** 4c3a builds recordkeeping and classification; 4c3b builds fast observation and comparison skills.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

{{< drg/tip >}}
If you already have safe, legal access to specimens from home, club meetings, or approved field trips, the collection path may be easier. If not, the identification path is a strong alternative.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
