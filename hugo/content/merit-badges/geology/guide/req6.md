{{< drg/requirement number="6" >}}
Do ONE of the following:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You must choose exactly one option for this final requirement. Both paths ask you to research and discuss geology beyond the badge, but they focus on different goals:

- **Req 6a** is about a professional path in geology or a related geoscience field.
- **Req 6b** is about using geology knowledge in a hobby, outdoor habit, or healthy long-term lifestyle.

## Your Options

- **[Req 6a — Geology Careers](/merit-badges/geology/guide/req6a/)**: Research one real geoscience career, including training, education, costs, salary, duties, and advancement. You will gain a practical look at how geology becomes a job.
- **[Req 6b — Geology as a Hobby or Lifestyle](/merit-badges/geology/guide/req6b/)**: Explore rockhounding, museum visits, hiking for geologic features, fossil clubs, or similar interests. You will gain a plan for keeping geology in your life even if it never becomes your profession.

## How to Choose

{{< drg/checklist title="Which Path Fits You?" subtitle="Use these questions to decide" >}}
- **Do you want to understand a job?** Choose 6a.
- **Do you want a hobby you can start now?** Choose 6b.
- **Do you enjoy interviews, research, and comparing education paths?** 6a may be more rewarding.
- **Do you enjoy field trips, collections, and outdoor exploration?** 6b may fit better.
- **What will you gain?** 6a builds career-planning skills; 6b builds a lifelong interest you can practice right away.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

{{< drg/tip >}}
If you already know you like science, engineering, or outdoor problem-solving, Req 6a can help you see where geology might fit. If you want to keep this badge fun and active after it ends, Req 6b may be the better choice.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

The finish line of the badge is close. Pick the path that feels most useful and interesting, then be ready to explain your thinking to your counselor.
