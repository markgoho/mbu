Do ONE of the following:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You must choose exactly one of these two paths:

- **Req 4b5a** asks you to create and share a short display or presentation.
- **Req 4b5b** asks you to arrange an approved visit to an operating drilling rig.

## Your Options

- **[Req 4b5a — Present How Fuels Are Found](/merit-badges/geology/guide/req4b5a/)**: Build a five-minute explanation of how oil, gas, or coal is found, extracted, and processed. This option is best if you like organizing information and teaching others.
- **[Req 4b5b — Visit a Drilling Rig](/merit-badges/geology/guide/req4b5b/)**: Observe real drilling work with parent or guardian and counselor permission. This option is best if you want first-hand exposure to industry work and field questions.

## How to Choose

{{< drg/checklist title="Presentation or Visit?" subtitle="Compare the two choices" >}}
- **Time and access:** A presentation is easier if you do not have a rig nearby.
- **Direct experience:** A visit gives you the strongest real-world view.
- **What you gain:** The presentation builds communication skills; the visit builds observation and interview skills.
- **Preparation needed:** Both require planning, but the visit depends on scheduling, permission, and site access.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

{{< drg/tip >}}
If an approved rig visit is realistic, it can be a memorable experience. If not, the presentation option still lets you show strong geology understanding by connecting discovery, extraction, and processing clearly.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
