{{< drg/requirement number="4c5a" >}}
With your parent or guardian's and counselor's approval, visit an active mining site, quarry, or sand and gravel pit. Tell your counselor what you learned about the resources extracted from this location and how these resources are used by society.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

A working extraction site makes geology feel immediate. You can see the material in place, the equipment used to remove it, and the huge scale needed to supply construction, manufacturing, or energy.

## What to Observe

- What material is being extracted?
- Is it blasted, cut, dredged, scooped, or screened?
- How is the material sorted or processed onsite?
- Where does it go after leaving the site?
- What products or infrastructure depend on it?

{{< drg/safety-first >}}
Active extraction sites are industrial workplaces. Follow all instructions, wear required safety gear, and stay only in approved visitor areas. Never collect specimens or approach equipment unless site staff specifically allow it.
{{< /drg/safety-first >}}
