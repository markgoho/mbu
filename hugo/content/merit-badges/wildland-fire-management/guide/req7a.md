## Suppression Tactics Table

| Tactic | Description | Primary Conditions / Best Used When |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[Direct Attack](/merit-badges/wildland-fire-management/guide/req7a1/)** | Constructing fireline directly at the burning edge of the fire. | Low flame lengths (< 4 ft), low intensity, accessible terrain. |
| **[Indirect Attack](/merit-badges/wildland-fire-management/guide/req7a2/)** | Constructing fireline well ahead of the fire and burning out intervening fuel. | High flame lengths (> 4 ft), fast-moving fires, dangerous terrain. |
| **[MIST Tactics](/merit-badges/wildland-fire-management/guide/req7a3/)** | Using sensitive suppression methods to minimize soil and stream damage. | Wilderness areas, national parks, sensitive habitat zones. |
| **[Crews & Aircraft](/merit-badges/wildland-fire-management/guide/req7a4/)** | Combining hand crews, bulldozers, water tenders, and air tankers. | Large complex fires requiring heavy equipment and aerial cooling. |
| **[Mop-Up](/merit-badges/wildland-fire-management/guide/req7a5/)** | Extinguishing all lingering heat and smoking embers near the fireline. | Post-containment to prevent rekindling and escape. |
| **[Repair & Restoration](/merit-badges/wildland-fire-management/guide/req7a6/)** | Installing water bars and reseeding firelines to prevent erosion. | Immediately following fire containment. |
