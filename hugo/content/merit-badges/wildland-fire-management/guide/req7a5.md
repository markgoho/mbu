Mop-up
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

**Mop-up** is the painstaking process of extinguishing or securing all burning embers, smoldering logs, and underground roots near the control line after the main fire front is contained.

### Key Mop-Up Steps

1. **Systematic Search**: Search a specified distance inside the control line (e.g., 50 to 100 feet) for smoking stumps, roots, or heat sources.
2. **Extinguish Heat**: Break up burning logs with McLeods or Pulaskis, mix hot embers with mineral soil, and douse thoroughly with water.
3. **Bare-Hand Test**: Use bare hands (caution!) to feel ashes and soil to guarantee zero lingering heat remains that could rekindle a fire.
