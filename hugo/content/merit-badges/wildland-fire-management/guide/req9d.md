{{< drg/requirement number="9d" option="Option D — Prevention Message Campaign" >}}
Meet with a Fire Prevention Specialist or a local fire official to learn about fire prevention efforts in your community. From what you learned, prepare a fire prevention message (skit, video, or billboard concept) and share with your counselor and troop (or other group approved by your counselor).
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Option D focuses on creative public communication and community fire safety outreach.

## Campaign Development Guide

1. **Fire Specialist Meeting**: Discuss local campfire rules, trailer safety chain hazards, and burn ban enforcement with a local fire specialist.
2. **Select a Medium**:
   - **Campfire Skit**: Write a 3-minute humorous or educational skit for your troop campfire demonstrating proper campfire drowning.
   - **Video PSA**: Produce a short video explaining how to create a 5-foot ember-resistant zone around homes.
   - **Poster / Billboard Concept**: Design an eye-catching poster highlighting local fire safety rules.
3. **Present Your Campaign**: Deliver your message to your troop or patrol and review the audience reaction with your counselor.
