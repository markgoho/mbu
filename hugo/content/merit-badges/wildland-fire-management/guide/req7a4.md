Use of hand crews, machinery, and aircraft
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Modern wildfire suppression relies on coordinated operations combining ground forces, heavy equipment, and aviation resources:

- **Hand Crews (Hotshots & Type 2 Crews)**: 18 to 20 firefighters equipped with hand tools (Pulaskis, McLeod tools, chainsaws) who dig firelines, cut brush, and conduct burnout operations in steep terrain.
- **Machinery (Bulldozers & Tractor-Plows)**: Heavy machinery used to rapidly scrape wide containment lines through dense brush and timber.
- **Aircraft (Air Tankers & Helitack)**: Fixed-wing air tankers drop long-term chemical fire retardant to slow fire spread, while helicopters (helitack) drop water buckets directly on hot spots to cool the fire for ground crews.
