Direct attack
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

A **direct attack** involves applying water, retardant, dirt, or handline construction directly at the burning edge of a wildfire.

### Tactical Execution & Conditions

- **Methods**: Firefighters scrape mineral dirt directly onto burning coals, smother flames with Pulaskis, or apply water hose lines directly to the flaming front.
- **Conditions**: Used when flame lengths are **less than 4 feet**, heat intensity is low, and smoke allows firefighters to work safely along the fire edge.
- **Advantages**: Minimizes acres burned and keeps the fire footprint small.
