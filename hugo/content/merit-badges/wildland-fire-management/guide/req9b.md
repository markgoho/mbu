{{< drg/requirement number="9b" option="Option B — Forestry Official Interview" >}}
Visit with a state or federal forestry official or your local fire warden. Discuss the causes of and types of wildland fire that occur in your area. Discuss the prime fire season(s) in your area. Identify the agency responsible for wildland fire suppression in your area. Discuss what you learned with your counselor.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Option B connects you with a local wildland fire professional.

## Interview Preparation Guide

Prepare questions prior to meeting with your local fire warden or forester:

1. **Responsible Agencies**: What local, state, or federal agency (e.g., State Forest Service, US Forest Service, BLM, or local volunteer fire department) handles primary wildland suppression in your area?
2. **Local Fire Seasons**: Which months represent peak fire season in your region, and how do seasonal weather patterns (Santa Ana winds, summer droughts, spring snowmelt) affect risk?
3. **Common Fire Causes**: What are the most frequent causes of wildfires in your specific county (debris burning, campfires, lightning, powerlines)?
4. **Local Prevention Efforts**: How does the agency conduct public education and fuel reduction programs locally?

After the interview, summarize your findings to share with your counselor.
