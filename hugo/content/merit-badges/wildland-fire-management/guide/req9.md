{{< drg/requirement number="9" >}}
Taking Action. Do ONE of the following:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Requirement 9 invites you to put wildland fire management into action through a practical project, interview, home assessment, or prevention campaign. You must complete **exactly ONE** of the following options.

## Option Summaries

- **[Req 9a — Fire Readiness Plan](/merit-badges/wildland-fire-management/guide/req9a/)**: Map a local wildland area and develop a complete fire readiness plan detailing water supplies, fuel loads, evacuation routes, and suppression tactics.
- **[Req 9b — Forestry Official Interview](/merit-badges/wildland-fire-management/guide/req9b/)**: Interview a local fire warden, state forester, or USFS official to discuss local fire seasons and suppression responsibility.
- **[Req 9c — Firewise Home Assessment & Risk Project](/merit-badges/wildland-fire-management/guide/req9c/)**: Conduct a formal Firewise assessment of a home or camp building in the WUI and complete an approved risk-reduction project.
- **[Req 9d — Community Fire Prevention Message](/merit-badges/wildland-fire-management/guide/req9d/)**: Meet with a Fire Prevention Specialist and create a skit, video, or billboard campaign to present to your troop.
- **[Req 9e — Fire Weather & Red Flag Warning Study](/merit-badges/wildland-fire-management/guide/req9e/)**: Meet with a meteorologist to learn about fire weather, relative humidity thresholds, and Red Flag Warnings.

## How to Choose

{{< drg/checklist title="Selecting Your Action Project" subtitle="Compare options to find your best fit" >}}
- **Req 9a (Readiness Mapping)**: Best if you enjoy mapping, spatial planning, and field scouting. *What you'll gain*: Practical emergency planning skills.
- **Req 9b (Forestry Interview)**: Best if you want to meet local wildland fire professionals. *What you'll gain*: Direct career insight into local agency operations.
- **Req 9c (Firewise Hands-on Project)**: Best if you want to perform hands-on fuel reduction around your home or Scout camp. *What you'll gain*: Practical home-hardening experience.
- **Req 9d (Creative Prevention Campaign)**: Best if you enjoy drama, video production, or graphic design. *What you'll gain*: Public communications and presentation skills.
- **Req 9e (Meteorology Focus)**: Best if you are interested in weather systems, humidity tracking, and atmospheric science. *What you'll gain*: Deep understanding of weather influences on fire.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

{{< drg/tip >}}
If your troop is planning an Eagle project, campsite service day, or Conservation project, Option 9c provides a fantastic service contribution for your local Scout camp!
{{< /drg/tip >}}
