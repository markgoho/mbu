## Requirement 4a1: Main Causes of Wildfire in the United States

{{< drg/requirement number="4a1" >}}
The main causes of wildfire in the United States
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Nearly **85% to 90% of wildfires in the United States are caused by humans**, while natural causes account for the remaining 10% to 15%.

### Primary Causes Breakdown

- **Human Causes (85–90%)**:
  - **Unattended Campfires**: Escaped embers from campfires that were not completely extinguished with water and dirt.
  - **Debris & Trash Burning**: Burning yard waste, leaves, or trash during high wind or low humidity warnings.
  - **Equipment Use & Sparks**: Dragging trailer safety chains on asphalt, lawnmower blades striking rocks, or hot exhaust pipes idling over dry grass.
  - **Discarded Smoking Materials**: Throwing lit cigarettes out of vehicle windows.
  - **Arson & Fireworks**: Intentional burning or illegal fireworks set off near dry vegetation.
- **Natural Causes (10–15%)**:
  - **Dry Lightning**: Lightning strikes from thunderclouds that produce rain that evaporates before reaching the ground (virga), igniting dry timber.
  - **Volcanic Activity**: Lava or hot pyroclastic flows igniting surrounding forests (rare).

## Requirement 4a2: Preventing Human-Caused Wildfires on Scouting Outings

{{< drg/requirement number="4a2" >}}
How you can prevent human caused wildfires in your community and on outings with your unit.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

{{< drg/checklist title="Campfire & Outing Wildfire Prevention" subtitle="Scout unit rules for campfire safety" >}}
- **1. Follow Fire Bans**: Always check local fire restrictions and burn bans before lighting any fire.
- **2. Clear a 10-Foot Perimeter**: Scrape away all leaves, twigs, and organic soil down to bare mineral dirt around your fire pit.
- **3. Douse, Stir, and Douse Again**: Extinguish campfires by drowning all embers with water, stirring the ashes with a shovel, and adding more water.
- **4. Test with the Back of Your Hand**: Never leave a fire pit until the coals are completely cold to the touch.
- **5. Keep Fuel Away from Heat**: Store liquid stove fuels and camp lantern gas at least 20 feet away from open flames.
- **6. Secure Trailer Safety Chains**: Ensure towing chains are hooked high enough so they do not drag and create sparks on highway pavement.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}
