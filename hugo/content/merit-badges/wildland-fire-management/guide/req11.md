Wildland fire management offers diverse career pathways spanning field operations, scientific research, aviation, and forestry administration.

## Career Pathways in Wildland Fire

### 1. Wildland Firefighter / Hotshot Crew Member
- **Duties**: Digging fireline, constructing fuel breaks, managing burnout operations, and performing mop-up.
- **Training & Education**: High school diploma, NWCG S-130/S-190 wildland certification, and rigorous physical pack test (45-lb pack over 3 miles in < 45 minutes).
- **Career Growth**: Crew Member → Squad Boss → Single Resource Boss → Crew Boss → Incident Commander.

### 2. Wildland Fire Ecologist / Forester
- **Duties**: Designing prescribed fire plans, studying plant regeneration post-fire, and managing forest health projects.
- **Training & Education**: Bachelor's degree in Forestry, Fire Ecology, Environmental Science, or Natural Resource Management.
- **Career Growth**: Junior Field Forester → District Silviculturist → Fire Management Officer (FMO).

### 3. Smokejumper / Helitack Specialist
- **Duties**: Parachuting into remote wilderness areas or rappelling from helicopters to provide initial attack on remote lightning fires.
- **Training & Education**: Minimum 1–2 years of hotshot crew experience, specialized parachute/rappelling training, and extreme physical conditioning.

### 4. Fire Prevention Specialist / Fire Warden
- **Duties**: Conducting community Firewise inspections, enforcing burn bans, and leading public fire safety education.
- **Training & Education**: Degree or coursework in Fire Science, Public Administration, or Forestry.
