The **Incident Command System (ICS)** is a standardized, on-scene emergency management system used nationwide by fire agencies, police, and emergency responders to coordinate resources during large-scale incidents.

### The ICS Organizational Structure

```
                  [ Incident Commander ]
                   /         |         \
         [ Public Info ] [ Safety ] [ Liaison ]
                            |
  -----------------------------------------------------
  |                 |                 |               |
[ Operations ]  [ Planning ]    [ Logistics ]   [ Finance/Admin ]
```

### Command Staff Positions

- **Incident Commander (IC)**: Overall authority for managing the entire incident, establishing safety objectives, and directing strategy.
- **Safety Officer**: Monitors all operations for hazardous conditions and has full authority to stop unsafe tactics immediately.
- **Public Information Officer (PIO)**: Serves as the official point of contact for news media, local communities, and public safety warnings.
- **Liaison Officer**: Coordinates with external assisting agencies (police, county road departments, utility providers, military).

### General Staff Positions

- **Operations Section Chief**: Directs all tactical ground and air firefighting operations (hand crews, dozers, engines, air tankers).
- **Planning Section Chief**: Collects weather forecasts, maps fire spread, tracks resources, and creates the daily Incident Action Plan (IAP).
- **Logistics Section Chief**: Provides facilities, food, water, medical support, transportation, and supplies to fire camps.
- **Finance / Administration Section Chief**: Tracks incident costs, vendor contracts, equipment rentals, and injury claims.
