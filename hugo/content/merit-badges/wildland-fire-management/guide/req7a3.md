Minimum impact suppression tactics
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

**Minimum Impact Suppression Tactics (MIST)** employ firefighting methods that accomplish suppression goals with the least possible physical, environmental, or cultural damage to the landscape.

### Key MIST Guidelines

- **Firelines**: Construct narrow handlines; flush-cut stumps level with the ground instead of uprooting trees; avoid using heavy bulldozers in wilderness zones.
- **Water Use**: Use water and natural wetting agents instead of persistent chemical retardants near streams and sensitive watersheds.
- **Camps & Travel**: Use low-impact camping practices, minimize tree cutting, and keep vehicle traffic restricted to established roads.
