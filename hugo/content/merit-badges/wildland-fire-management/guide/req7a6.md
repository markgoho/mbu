Repair and restoration
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

**Post-fire suppression repair** takes place immediately after fire containment to repair damages caused by firefighting activities and prevent post-fire erosion.

### Key Repair Actions

- **Water Bars**: Construct earthen berms (water bars) diagonally across firelines on slopes to divert rainwater into stable vegetation, preventing gully erosion.
- **Ripping & Mulching**: De-compact bulldozer trails and spread straw or wood mulch over exposed dirt.
- **Reseeding**: Plant native grass and seedling species to stabilize soil and prevent invasive weeds from establishing.
