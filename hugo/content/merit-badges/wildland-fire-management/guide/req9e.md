Option E explores atmospheric science and National Weather Service fire weather warnings.

## Fire Weather Discussion Topics

- **Relative Humidity (RH)**: Understand why RH dropping below **15% to 20%** drastically increases fuel flammability.
- **Wind Speed & Gusts**: Sustained winds above 20 mph rapidly accelerate fire fronts and carry spot embers.
- **Haines Index**: Measures atmospheric instability and dry air promoting plume-dominated megafires.
- **NWS Warnings**:
  - **Fire Weather Watch**: Issued 24 to 72 hours in advance when weather conditions favor critical fire growth.
  - **Red Flag Warning**: Issued within 24 hours when warm temperatures, very low humidity, and strong winds combine to produce extreme fire danger.
