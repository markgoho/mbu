{{< drg/requirement number="4" >}}
Under the supervision of your parent, guardian, or counselor, do TWO of the following:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Electricity is the part of home repair where confidence must stay matched to caution. You must choose exactly **two** of these options, and every one starts with the same first step: make the circuit safe before touching any electrical part.

## Your Options

- **[Req 4a — Breakers and Fuses](/merit-badges/home-repairs/guide/req4a/)**: Learn where the main electrical panel is and how to reset or replace protection devices. You will gain system awareness and emergency readiness.
- **[Req 4b — Cords, Plugs, and Sockets](/merit-badges/home-repairs/guide/req4b/)**: Repair a plug, lamp socket, or cord. You will learn safe disassembly and reconnection of common household parts.
- **[Req 4c — Installing a Light Switch](/merit-badges/home-repairs/guide/req4c/)**: Replace a basic single-pole switch. You will learn orderly wiring and careful device installation.
- **[Req 4d — Replacing an Outlet](/merit-badges/home-repairs/guide/req4d/)**: Replace a wall outlet. You will learn secure terminal connections and box safety.

## How to Choose

{{< drg/checklist title="Choosing Your Electrical Options" subtitle="Pick the two that best fit your home and supervision" >}}
- **Best starting point:** Req 4a. Everyone should know where the main switch box is and how to respond to a tripped breaker.
- **Best small-device repair:** Req 4b. Good for learning wiring concepts on something portable and familiar.
- **Best wall-switch project:** Req 4c. Useful if your home has a simple switch needing replacement.
- **Best outlet project:** Req 4d. Good if a worn outlet cover or receptacle needs supervised replacement.
- **What you will gain:** These projects build respect for shutdown procedures, wire organization, and why correct connections matter.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

{{< drg/safety-first >}}
Never work on an energized circuit. Turn off the correct breaker, verify power is actually off, and keep hands, tools, and surfaces dry. If a device box looks scorched, brittle, or confusing, stop and get adult guidance before going further.
{{< /drg/safety-first >}}
