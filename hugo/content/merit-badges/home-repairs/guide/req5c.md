## What to Observe

A toilet repair often starts with asking:

- Is it running constantly?
- Is it failing to refill?
- Is it not flushing completely?
- Is the chain, flapper, or fill mechanism the likely problem?

Watch the tank through one full flush cycle. That will often tell you what is not closing, lifting, or refilling correctly.

{{< drg/image src="images/toilet-flush-cycle-parts.avif" alt="Cutaway toilet tank diagram showing the handle, chain, flapper, fill valve, float, and flush sequence" >}}

{{< drg/be-prepared title="If the Problem Is Outside the Tank" >}}
You expect a simple tank repair but find water on the floor.

- Stop and inspect where the water is coming from.
- Check whether the shutoff valve, supply line, or another connection may be leaking.
- Tell your supervising adult before continuing, because the problem may no longer be just a flush issue.
{{< /drg/be-prepared >}}

Toilet repair is a great example of how observation saves time. If you understand the sequence, you can focus on the part that failed instead of replacing things blindly.
