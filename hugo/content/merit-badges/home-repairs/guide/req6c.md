{{< drg/requirement number="6c" >}}
Install drapery or curtain rods and then hang drapes or curtains.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

A curtain rod that is even slightly crooked is easy to notice every time you enter the room. That is why this requirement is really about layout and measuring, not just putting brackets on a wall.

{{< drg/video
    title="How to Hang Curtain Rods (video)"
    url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bJr6jPYs5_I" >}}

## Skills This Project Builds

- Measuring left and right placement evenly
- Checking level before drilling or mounting
- Choosing a mounting point strong enough for the rod and fabric
- Testing how the curtain hangs and moves once installed

{{< drg/tip >}}
Measure from the same reference point on both sides of the window. Guessing from the ceiling or trim by eye is how rods end up visibly uneven.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
