{{< drg/requirement number="6g" >}}
Mend an object made of china, glass, or pottery.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Fragile repairs demand a different attitude from construction projects. Force does not help. Speed does not help. You succeed by handling pieces gently, understanding how they fit, and giving adhesives the time they need.

## What This Requirement Teaches

- Align pieces before applying adhesive
- Work on a protected surface where pieces cannot roll or slide
- Keep track of all fragments so the repair stays complete
- Let the repair cure fully before testing it

{{< drg/safety-first >}}
If the broken object has sharp edges, wear gloves and handle each piece slowly. Broken ceramics and glass can cut just as badly during cleanup as during the original break.
{{< /drg/safety-first >}}

This project echoes [Req 2f — Replacing a Window Pane](/merit-badges/home-repairs/guide/req2f/) in one important way: fragile materials punish rushing.
