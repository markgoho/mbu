Reinforce a picture frame.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Picture frames often fail at the corners because that is where the parts meet and where the whole frame resists twisting. Reinforcing a frame teaches a compact version of a bigger structural lesson: corners matter.

## What to Look For

- Are the corners opening up?
- Is the backing still fitting squarely?
- Does the frame rack or twist when handled?

A good reinforcement keeps the frame aligned without making the repair look clumsy or uneven.

{{< drg/image src="images/picture-frame-corner-reinforcement.avif" alt="Failing picture-frame corner compared with a neatly reinforced square corner" >}}

{{< drg/tip >}}
Dry-fit any reinforcing pieces or hardware before attaching them permanently. A small object can still be ruined by rushing the layout.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
