{{< drg/requirement number="9" option="Single A-Frame Bridge" >}}
Working in a group, (or individually with the help of your counselor) build a full size pioneering structure, using one of the following designs in the Pioneering merit badge pamphlet:Carefully plan the project, assembling and organizing all the materials, referring to the points under Safe Pioneering, and complying with the height restrictions in the Guide to Safe Scouting.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

The Single A-Frame Bridge keeps the bridge idea but simplifies the support system compared with the double A-frame monkey bridge. That makes it a solid choice for a team that wants a major structure without the extra complexity of a larger bridge setup.

### What you learn here

- How A-frame supports spread force into the ground
- How straight alignment affects the feel of the crossing
- How careful anchoring turns a narrow design into a stable one

{{< drg/be-prepared title="Your bridge support leans during setup" >}}
- **Stop the raise immediately.**
- **Check that both frame legs are even and on stable ground.**
- **Inspect the lashings and braces before trying again.**
- **Reset with better spacing instead of forcing it upright.**
{{< /drg/be-prepared >}}

{{< drg/tip >}}
Choose this option if your team wants to study bridge structure but is not ready for the largest or most complex bridge in the list.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
