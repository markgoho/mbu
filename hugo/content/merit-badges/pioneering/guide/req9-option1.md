{{< drg/requirement number="9" option="Double A-Frame Monkey Bridge" >}}
Working in a group, (or individually with the help of your counselor) build a full size pioneering structure, using one of the following designs in the Pioneering merit badge pamphlet:Carefully plan the project, assembling and organizing all the materials, referring to the points under Safe Pioneering, and complying with the height restrictions in the Guide to Safe Scouting.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

A monkey bridge is one of the classic pioneering projects because it looks exciting and teaches a lot at once. It combines elevated supports, strong anchoring, hand lines, foot lines, and real moving loads as people cross.

## What makes this option challenging

This bridge teaches how a structure behaves when the load is not static. The force changes with each person's movement, so alignment, anchors, and inspection matter more than looks.

{{< drg/checklist title="Plan before you build" subtitle="Questions to answer with your team" >}}
- **Do we have enough room for the span and approach areas?**
- **Do we have enough qualified adult supervision for a bridge project?**
- **Are our anchors and A-frames appropriate for the expected load?**
- **Who is checking the bridge before anyone crosses?**
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

{{< drg/safety-first >}}
Do not let anyone cross until your counselor or another qualified adult has inspected and approved the finished bridge. A bridge is never the place to "test it by seeing what happens."
{{< /drg/safety-first >}}
