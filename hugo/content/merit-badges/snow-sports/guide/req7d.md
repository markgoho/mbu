Option D—Snowshoeing. Do ALL of the following:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You have chosen snowshoeing. This is the most accessible option — snowshoeing requires no special technique, no lifts, and no groomed trails. If there is snow on the ground and you can walk, you can snowshoe.

Work through all ten requirements below. The first nine are knowledge and skills that you can prepare for before and during your hike; the final requirement (7d10) adds navigation skills using a topographic map and compass.

## What You'll Complete

- **[Req 7d1 — Parts of a Snowshoe](/merit-badges/snow-sports/guide/req7d1/)**: Name the parts of a snowshoe.
- **[Req 7d2 — Choosing the Right Size](/merit-badges/snow-sports/guide/req7d2/)**: Explain how to choose the correct snowshoe size.
- **[Req 7d3 — Types & Specialized Uses](/merit-badges/snow-sports/guide/req7d3/)**: Describe the types of snowshoes and the factors to consider when choosing one.
- **[Req 7d4 — Care & Maintenance](/merit-badges/snow-sports/guide/req7d4/)**: Explain how to care for and maintain snowshoes.
- **[Req 7d5 — One-Day Hike Gear](/merit-badges/snow-sports/guide/req7d5/)**: List the items to take on a one-day snowshoe hike.
- **[Req 7d6 — Best Areas & Backcountry](/merit-badges/snow-sports/guide/req7d6/)**: Describe good snowshoeing areas and discuss backcountry advantages and dangers.
- **[Req 7d7 — Benefits of Snowshoeing](/merit-badges/snow-sports/guide/req7d7/)**: Discuss the benefits of snowshoeing as an activity.
- **[Req 7d8 — Trail Techniques](/merit-badges/snow-sports/guide/req7d8/)**: Demonstrate efficient trail breaking, uphill climbing, downhill travel, and traversing.
- **[Req 7d9 — 2-Mile Snowshoe Hike](/merit-badges/snow-sports/guide/req7d9/)**: Complete a 2-mile hike demonstrating ability to handle varied snow conditions.
- **[Req 7d10 — Map & Compass](/merit-badges/snow-sports/guide/req7d-10/)**: Demonstrate proper use of a topographic map and compass.
