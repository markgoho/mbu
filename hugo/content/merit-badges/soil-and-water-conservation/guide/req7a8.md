{{< drg/requirement number="7a8" >}}
Make a trip to an industry water use installation. Write a report of more than 500 words about the soil and water and energy conservation practices you saw.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

This option helps you see that conservation is not only about farms and streams. Many industries rely on water for cooling, cleaning, processing, and product quality. The interesting part of the visit is how the facility reduces waste and protects nearby water.

Look for closed-loop cooling, reuse systems, monitoring equipment, treatment units, leak detection, and efforts to reduce both water and energy use.

{{< drg/be-prepared title="What to Watch For at an Industrial Site" >}}
Industrial visits can move fast, so keep your notes focused.

- Where does the water come from?
- Which parts of the process use the most water?
- What gets recycled or reused?
- How is wastewater treated or monitored before discharge?
- Where do water savings also reduce energy use?
{{< /drg/be-prepared >}}
