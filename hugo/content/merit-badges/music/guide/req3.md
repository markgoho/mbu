{{< drg/requirement number="3" >}}
Do TWO of the following:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

This requirement gives you four different ways to engage with music. You will choose exactly **two** options, so pick the pair that matches your interests, schedule, and access to performances or groups.

## Your Options

- **[Req 3a — Listen and Describe](/merit-badges/music/guide/req3a/)**: Attend a live performance or listen to three hours of music from two styles, then describe what you heard and how it affected you. This option builds listening vocabulary and helps you notice musical details.
- **[Req 3b — Family Music Interview](/merit-badges/music/guide/req3b/)**: Compare your favorite music with a relative's favorites from another generation. This option teaches you how music connects to memory, identity, and change over time.
- **[Req 3c — Ensemble or Solo Service](/merit-badges/music/guide/req3c/)**: Commit to six months in a musical group or six public solo performances. This option builds discipline, teamwork, and stage confidence.
- **[Req 3d — Influential Americans in Music](/merit-badges/music/guide/req3d/)**: Research five important Americans in music history and explain why they still matter. This option builds historical understanding and helps you connect people to lasting changes in music.

## How to Choose

{{< drg/checklist title="Choosing Your Two Options" subtitle="Think about what you can realistically do well" >}}
- **Time available:** Req 3c takes the longest because it unfolds over six months. Req 3a, 3b, and 3d can usually be completed more quickly.
- **Access to events or people:** Req 3a works best if you can attend a concert or easily find recordings. Req 3b depends on a willing adult family member. Req 3c depends on access to a group or public performance opportunities.
- **Your strengths:** If you love discussion and observation, start with 3a or 3b. If you like history and research, 3d may fit well. If you learn best by doing, 3c may be the strongest choice.
- **What you will gain:** Req 3a sharpens your ears, 3b deepens personal connection, 3c develops musicianship through repetition, and 3d shows how people reshape the country's musical life.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

A good pair often balances one reflective option with one active option. For example, 3a plus 3c combines listening with performance, while 3b plus 3d combines personal conversation with historical research.

{{< drg/tip >}}
If you are already in a band, choir, or regular music program, Req 3c may be an easy fit. If you are newer to music, Req 3a and Req 3b can be excellent starting points because they focus on listening and conversation.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
