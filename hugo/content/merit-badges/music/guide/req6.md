{{< drg/requirement number="6" >}}
Do ONE of the following:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You must choose exactly **one** option here. Both choices ask you to think beyond the badge and imagine how music could stay part of your life after this merit badge is done.

## Your Options

- **[Req 6a — Research a Music Career](/merit-badges/music/guide/req6a/)**: Study one real career connected to music, including education, cost, job duties, advancement, and salary. This option helps you understand what professional life in music can actually look like.
- **[Req 6b — Turn Music into a Hobby](/merit-badges/music/guide/req6b/)**: Explore how music knowledge and skills could grow into a long-term hobby, including goals, costs, training, and organizations. This option helps you build a realistic personal plan.

## How to Choose

{{< drg/checklist title="Choosing Between Career and Hobby" subtitle="Pick the option that answers the question you are most curious about" >}}
- **Choose 6a if:** you want to know how people earn a living in music and what preparation that path requires.
- **Choose 6b if:** you want to keep music in your life for enjoyment, service, creativity, or community without making it your job.
- **Time and research:** both options require real research, but 6a usually asks for more career-specific facts like salary and advancement.
- **What you will gain:** 6a builds career awareness and realistic expectations. 6b builds self-direction and a practical plan for continued involvement.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

Some Scouts discover that music is something they love but do not want as a career. Others discover the opposite. Either result is useful. The purpose is to think carefully and honestly.

{{< drg/tip >}}
If you already know a musician, music teacher, sound engineer, or arts leader, Req 6a can be especially strong because a real interview makes the career picture much clearer. If you mainly want to keep music in your life while exploring other careers, Req 6b may be the better fit.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

[Req 3c](/merit-badges/music/guide/req3c/) and [Req 4](/merit-badges/music/guide/req4/) can help you here. The experiences you enjoyed most in those sections often point toward the future path that fits you best.
