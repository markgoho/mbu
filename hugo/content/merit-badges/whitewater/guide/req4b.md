Option B—Solo Canoest. Do ALL of the following strokes and techniques on calm water:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Work through each child requirement below in order. Solo canoeing demands efficiency because there is no partner to fix a late stroke or add extra power.

## What You'll Complete

- **[Req 4b1 — Solo Canoe Precision Strokes](/merit-badges/whitewater/guide/req4b1/)**: Practice the full stroke set needed to drive, correct, and place a solo canoe accurately.
- **[Req 4b2 — Bracing a Solo Canoe](/merit-badges/whitewater/guide/req4b2/)**: Practice support strokes that keep the canoe upright when the hull gets knocked off balance.

{{< drg/tip >}}
Solo canoe control is often about making smaller, earlier corrections. If you wait until the boat is badly off line, you usually have to work much harder to fix it.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
