You must choose exactly one option from this requirement. Boat design matters because hull shape, outfitting, and materials all affect how a craft turns, resurfaces, carries gear, and handles hits from rocks.

## Your Options

- **[Req 7a — Whitewater Canoe Designs](/merit-badges/whitewater/guide/req7a/)**: Learn how moving-water canoes vary in openness, rocker, hull shape, and intended use. This option helps canoe paddlers choose a boat that fits tandem or solo river travel.
- **[Req 7b — Whitewater Kayak Designs](/merit-badges/whitewater/guide/req7b/)**: Learn how kayaks vary by volume, length, maneuverability, and purpose. This option helps kayakers connect boat shape to river style and skill goals.

## How to Choose

{{< drg/checklist title="Choosing the right boat-design page" subtitle="Pick the option that matches the craft you will actually paddle" >}}
- **Canoe path**: Best if you are earning Whitewater in an open canoe and need to understand canoe-specific tradeoffs like tandem space, solo control, and flotation setup.
- **Kayak path**: Best if you are earning Whitewater in a decked kayak and need to understand how different whitewater kayak styles behave.
- **What you will gain**: You will be better at selecting appropriate equipment instead of assuming every boat works for every run.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

{{< drg/tip >}}
The best whitewater boat is not automatically the most advanced-looking one. It is the one that matches the river, the paddler, and the goals of the trip.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
