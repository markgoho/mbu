{{< drg/requirement number="3" >}}
Before doing requirements 4 through 12, earn the [Canoeing merit badge](/merit-badges/canoeing/) if you will be using a canoe to earn this merit badge. If you will be using a kayak, earn the [Kayaking merit badge](/merit-badges/kayaking/). Then do ONE of the following:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You must choose exactly one option from this requirement. This is really a commitment point: pick the craft you will use for the badge, make sure you have already earned the matching flatwater badge, and then prove that your core strokes are ready for moving water.

## Your Options

- **[Req 3a — Canoe Skills Check](/merit-badges/whitewater/guide/req3a/)**: Review canoe strokes and maneuvers from the [Canoeing merit badge](/merit-badges/canoeing/) so your counselor can see that you already have dependable boat control. This path is for Scouts completing Whitewater in either a tandem or solo canoe.
- **[Req 3b — Kayak Skills Check](/merit-badges/whitewater/guide/req3b/)**: Review strokes and maneuvers from the [Kayaking merit badge](/merit-badges/kayaking/) so you can build whitewater technique on a solid kayak foundation. This path is for Scouts completing Whitewater in a solo kayak.

## How to Choose

{{< drg/checklist title="Choosing your craft path" subtitle="Think beyond what looks coolest in the photo" >}}
- **Previous training**: Choose the path that matches the merit badge you have already earned—canoeing or kayaking.
- **Boat feel**: Canoes offer more open space and different paddle mechanics; kayaks offer closer body contact and quicker edging response.
- **What you will gain**: Canoe paths build teamwork or solo open-boat control; the kayak path sharpens fast hull response and decked-boat handling.
- **What comes next**: Your choice determines which calm-water strokes and braces you will practice in [Req 4](/merit-badges/whitewater/guide/req4/).
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

{{< drg/tip >}}
Do not choose based on what a friend is paddling this weekend. Choose the craft you can practice consistently with a qualified adult, proper outfitting, and enough time on the water to become comfortable.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
