Option C—Muzzleloading Rifle Shooting. Do ALL of the following:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Option C introduces you to one of the oldest forms of rifle shooting—the muzzleloading rifle. Unlike cartridges that contain all components in a single unit, a muzzleloader is loaded from the front of the barrel with separate charges of black powder, wad, and projectile. This is the technology that shaped American history, and it remains an active sport and hunting tradition today.

Option C is the most involved of the three options. It has fourteen sub-requirements covering history, mechanics, propellant safety, loading procedures, firing failure responses, range procedures, cleaning, and two live-fire exercises.

## What You'll Complete

- **[Req 2ca — History of Muzzleloading Rifles](/merit-badges/rifle-shooting/guide/req2ca/)**: A brief history of muzzleloader development from flintlock to percussion cap.
- **[Req 2cb — Principal Parts and How They Work](/merit-badges/rifle-shooting/guide/req2cb/)**: Identify and explain the major parts of a muzzleloading rifle.
- **[Req 2cc — Safe Storage and Handling](/merit-badges/rifle-shooting/guide/req2cc/)**: Know the rules for storing and handling a muzzleloader safely.
- **[Req 2cd — Grades of Black Powder](/merit-badges/rifle-shooting/guide/req2cd/)**: Identify the different grades and explain their uses.
- **[Req 2ce — Black Powder Storage Safety](/merit-badges/rifle-shooting/guide/req2ce/)**: Understand the specific requirements for storing black powder safely.
- **[Req 2cf — Components of a Load](/merit-badges/rifle-shooting/guide/req2cf/)**: Discuss the powder charge, patch, and ball or conical projectile.
- **[Req 2cg — Loading Procedures and Accessories](/merit-badges/rifle-shooting/guide/req2cg/)**: Identify the tools and correct sequence for loading safely.
- **[Req 2ch — Misfires, Hangfires, and Squib Fires](/merit-badges/rifle-shooting/guide/req2ch/)**: Understand failure modes unique to muzzleloaders and how to respond.
- **[Req 2ci — Range Procedures and Commands](/merit-badges/rifle-shooting/guide/req2ci/)**: Demonstrate safe range behavior for muzzleloader shooting.
- **[Req 2cj — Cleaning Safety Rules and Materials](/merit-badges/rifle-shooting/guide/req2cj/)**: Know the safety rules and materials for cleaning a muzzleloader.
- **[Req 2ck — Clean a Muzzleloader Properly](/merit-badges/rifle-shooting/guide/req2ck/)**: Demonstrate the cleaning process for a muzzleloading rifle.
- **[Req 2cl — Selecting a Muzzleloading Rifle](/merit-badges/rifle-shooting/guide/req2cl/)**: Discuss the factors for choosing a muzzleloader.
- **[Req 2cm — First Grouping Exercise](/merit-badges/rifle-shooting/guide/req2cm/)**: Fire three three-shot groups at 50 feet that fit under a soft drink can base.
- **[Req 2cn — Scored Grouping Exercise](/merit-badges/rifle-shooting/guide/req2cn/)**: Fire three five-shot groups meeting minimum score thresholds.

## Before You Begin

Muzzleloading requires a range equipped for black powder, with appropriate ventilation and fire safety measures. Confirm with your counselor that the range is set up for this option before proceeding.
