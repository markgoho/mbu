Silversmithing is the precision branch of this badge. Instead of shaping heavier stock with big hammer blows, you work with thinner material, smaller tools, tighter cuts, and cleaner joints. This path rewards patience, accuracy, and a good eye for finish.

## What You'll Complete

- **[Req 5b1 — Silversmith Tools](/merit-badges/metalwork/guide/req5b1/)**: Learn the small, precise tools used in silversmithing.
- **[Req 5b2 — Sketching Your Silver Objects](/merit-badges/metalwork/guide/req5b2/)**: Plan two objects with enough clarity to build them cleanly.
- **[Req 5b3 — Sawing, Sinking & Soldering](/merit-badges/metalwork/guide/req5b3/)**: Build two objects with soldered joints and include required hand-made features.

## How This Path Builds

This option teaches control. First you identify the toolset. Then you plan carefully. Then you make two objects that prove you can cut, shape, join, and finish metal without rushing.

{{< drg/tip >}}
Silversmithing often looks easy from a distance because the finished work is small and neat. Up close, it demands careful sawing, good heat control, and a willingness to slow down.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
