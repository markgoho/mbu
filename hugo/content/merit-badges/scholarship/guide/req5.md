{{< drg/requirement number="5" >}}
Do ONE of the following:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

This final requirement asks you to put your thinking into writing. You must complete exactly **one** option, and both choices ask you to connect school to the future in a thoughtful, personal way.

## Your Options

- **[Req 5a — Your Education Story](/merit-badges/scholarship/guide/req5a/)**: Write about how the education you receive now will matter later, and how you plan to keep learning in the future.
- **[Req 5b — Careers and Classes](/merit-badges/scholarship/guide/req5b/)**: Write about two careers that interest you and how specific classes and good scholarship can help you reach them.

## How to Choose

| Option | Best fit for | Main writing focus | What you will gain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Req 5a | A Scout ready to reflect personally on learning | Your education, your future, and lifelong learning | Clearer thinking about why school matters to you |
| Req 5b | A Scout already curious about possible careers | Two career paths and the classes that support them | Stronger links between school subjects and future goals |

{{< drg/checklist title="Pick the Easier Honest Topic" subtitle="The best choice is the one you can discuss specifically" >}}
- **Choose Req 5a** if you have strong ideas about how education shapes your future, even if your career plans are still broad.
- **Choose Req 5b** if you already have two career ideas and can connect them to classes or skills.
- **Remember the word count**: Both reports need 250 to 300 words, so you must be focused.
- **What you will gain**: Req 5a builds reflection. Req 5b builds planning and career awareness.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

{{< drg/tip >}}
Do not pick the option that sounds more impressive. Pick the one that lets you say something real with examples.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

Both choices work best if you draw on earlier parts of the badge. Interviews from [Req 2b](/merit-badges/scholarship/guide/req2b/) may help with career ideas. Time-management and study habits from [Req 2c](/merit-badges/scholarship/guide/req2c/) can help you explain what scholarship looks like in practice.
