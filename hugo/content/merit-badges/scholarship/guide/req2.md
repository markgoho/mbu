{{< drg/requirement number="2" >}}
Do TWO of the following:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

This requirement covers four different ways to become a stronger learner. You must complete exactly **two** options, and each one develops a useful skill you can keep using long after this badge is finished.

## Your Options

- **[Req 2a — Learn From Local Places](/merit-badges/scholarship/guide/req2a/)**: Explore educational places near where you live, visit one, and report on what you learned through the experience.
- **[Req 2b — Interview Working Adults](/merit-badges/scholarship/guide/req2b/)**: Talk with two professionals about their education, training, and how learning continues after school ends.
- **[Req 2c — Use a Planner Well](/merit-badges/scholarship/guide/req2c/)**: Show how you track assignments and activities, then explain how you manage your time.
- **[Req 2d — Compare Research Methods](/merit-badges/scholarship/guide/req2d/)**: Discuss the strengths and weaknesses of libraries, print sources, internet research, and newer tools such as AI.

## How to Choose

| Option | Best fit for | Time and setup | What you will gain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Req 2a | A Scout who likes learning by exploring | Requires a visit away from home or school | Practice turning a place into a learning experience |
| Req 2b | A Scout curious about careers | Requires approvals and scheduling interviews | Learn how education connects to real work |
| Req 2c | A Scout who wants stronger organization | Uses tools you may already have | Build time-management habits you can use every week |
| Req 2d | A Scout who does research often | Mostly discussion-based | Learn how to choose the right source for the task |

{{< drg/checklist title="A Smart Way to Pick Two" subtitle="Think about both convenience and value" >}}
- **Choose one practical habit**: Req 2c or Req 2d gives you a skill you can use in almost every class.
- **Choose one outside-the-classroom experience**: Req 2a or Req 2b pushes you to learn from the world beyond school walls.
- **Consider access**: If transportation or scheduling is hard, Req 2c and Req 2d may be easier to complete quickly.
- **Think ahead**: If you are also working on [Req 5b](/merit-badges/scholarship/guide/req5b/), Req 2b can give you ideas for careers to write about later.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

{{< drg/tip >}}
There is no single best pair of choices. The best combination is the one that teaches you something useful and that you can complete honestly with good evidence.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

By the time you finish your two options, you should know more about how you learn, where you can find knowledge, and how to manage your work like a stronger student.
