{{< drg/requirement number="6" >}}
Describe the purpose, operation, and components of ONE of the following:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You must choose exactly one option from this requirement. Both choices focus on how humans live and work in orbit, but they do it at different scales.

## Your Options

- **[Req 6a — Crewed Orbital Vehicles](/merit-badges/space-exploration/guide/req6a/)**: Study a shuttle, capsule, or other crewed orbital vehicle. This option helps you explain how a spacecraft carries people to orbit, supports them briefly, and returns them safely.
- **[Req 6b — International Space Station](/merit-badges/space-exploration/guide/req6b/)**: Focus on the ISS as a long-term orbital home and laboratory. This option is great if you want to explain how people live, work, and cooperate in space over time.

## How to Choose

{{< drg/checklist title="Choosing your option" subtitle="Pick the one that fits what you want to describe" >}}

- **Choose 6a if** you are most interested in launches, reentry, crews, and spacecraft systems packed into one vehicle.
- **Choose 6b if** you are most interested in long-duration living, science experiments, and international teamwork.
- **What you gain from 6a**: Better understanding of how spacecraft transport humans.
- **What you gain from 6b**: Better understanding of how humans stay alive and productive in orbit.
  {{< /drg/checklist >}}

{{< drg/tip >}}
If you already liked the orbit and life-support ideas in Requirements 4 and 7, the ISS option may feel natural. If you prefer vehicles and launch systems, start with the crewed vehicle page.
{{< /drg/tip >}}
