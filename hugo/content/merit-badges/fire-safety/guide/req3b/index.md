## Key Points

**Fabric Types and Safety:**
- Synthetic fabrics (polyester) melt and cause deeper burns
- Natural fibers (cotton, wool) burn but don't melt
- Tight-fitting clothes are safer than loose
- Leather and treated fabrics offer protection

**Prevention Strategies:**
- Wear fitted clothing around open flames
- Avoid synthetic fabrics near heat sources
- Keep long sleeves and hair tied back while cooking
- Use proper protective equipment in workshops

**How Clothing Affects Burn Severity:**
- Melting synthetics create deeper burns
- Loose garments catch fire more easily
- Multiple layers provide insulation
- Fire-resistant clothing reduces injury severity
