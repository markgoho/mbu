title: "Eagle Required Merit Badges"
description: "The merit badges required for the Eagle Scout rank in Scouting America"
type: "eagle-required"
---

To earn the Eagle Scout rank, a Scout must earn a total of 21 merit badges, including 14 specific required badges: 10 that every Eagle Scout must earn, plus one from each of 3 "choose one" groups.
