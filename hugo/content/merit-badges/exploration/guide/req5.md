{{< drg/requirement number="5" >}}
Exploration in Lab and Field. Do ONE of the following, and share what you learn with your counselor:

a. With your parent or guardian's permission and counselor's approval, visit either in person or via the internet an exploration sponsoring organization (such as The Explorers Club, National Geographic Society, Smithsonian Institution, American Alpine Club, World Wildlife Fund, or similar organization). Find out what type(s) of exploration the organization supports.

b. With permission and approval, visit either in person or via the internet a science lab, astronomical observatory, medical research facility, or similar site. Learn what exploration is done in this facility.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

This requirement takes your learning into the real world. You will visit either an organization that supports exploration or a research facility where exploration happens. Choose **one** option and go deeper.

## Option Overview

### Option A: Exploration Organization Visit

Visit (in person or online) an organization dedicated to funding, supporting, or conducting exploration. Examples:
- **The Explorers Club** (explorers.org) — Supports explorers and scientific expeditions
- **National Geographic Society** (nationalgeographic.org/society/) — Funds geographic and scientific exploration
- **Smithsonian Institution** (si.edu) — Operates museums and funds research
- **World Wildlife Fund** (worldwildlife.org) — Supports wildlife and habitat exploration
- **American Alpine Club** (americanalpineclub.org) — Supports mountain exploration

Learn what kinds of expeditions they support, how they decide which explorers to fund, and what impact their work has.

### Option B: Research Facility Visit

Visit (in person or online) a research laboratory where exploration actually happens. Examples:
- **University biology or chemistry lab**
- **Hospital research center** (cancer research, neuroscience, etc.)
- **Astronomical observatory**
- **Aquarium marine research facility**
- **Geological survey office**

Learn what questions researchers are investigating and how they conduct their work.
