{{< drg/requirement number="6d" >}}
Determine equipment and supplies required for personal and mission needs for the length of the expedition.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

What goes in your pack? This requirement asks you to identify everything you will need — both personal items for survival/comfort and specialized equipment for your exploration objectives.

## Personal Gear & Supplies

Every expedition member needs:

**Shelter & Sleep:**
- Tent or shelter
- Sleeping bag
- Sleeping pad
- Pillow

**Clothing:**
- Base layers
- Insulating layers
- Rain jacket & pants
- Extra socks
- Hat & gloves (if cold climate)
- Footwear appropriate to terrain

**Food & Water:**
- Meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner)
- High-energy snacks
- Water bottles or hydration pack
- Water purification method (filter, tablets, or boiling)

**First Aid & Safety:**
- First aid kit
- Medications (prescription, pain relievers, anti-diarrhea, etc.)
- Sunscreen & insect repellent
- Blister treatment
- Emergency shelter or emergency bivy

**Navigation & Communication:**
- Map of the area
- Compass
- GPS device or smartphone with offline maps
- Whistle
- Headlamp & extra batteries
- Satellite communicator or personal locator beacon (if remote)

**Hygiene & Health:**
- Toilet paper & waste bags
- Hand sanitizer
- Toothbrush & toothpaste
- Soap
- Feminine hygiene products (if needed)
- Towel or bandana

**Tools & Repair:**
- Multi-tool or knife
- Duct tape
- Rope or cord
- Repair kit for tent and gear

## Mission-Specific Equipment

These depend on your exploration objective:

### For Ecological/Biological Surveys

- **Field guides** (species identification books)
- **Binoculars** (for bird/wildlife observation)
- **Field notebook & pencils** (waterproof)
- **Camera** with telephoto lens
- **Collection containers** (nets, bags, jars)
- **Measuring tape & ruler**
- **Thermometer**
- **Hand lens or magnifying glass**
- **GPS device** (for recording locations)
- **Specimen preservation supplies** (alcohol, labels, specimen jars)

### For Geology/Archaeological Exploration

- **Geological hammer & chisel**
- **Rock/mineral field guide**
- **Hand lens or magnifying glass**
- **Compass** (for measuring strike and dip)
- **GPS device**
- **Camera**
- **Sample bags & labels**
- **Measuring tools** (tape, ruler, calipers)
- **Notebook for detailed observations**
- **Trowel** (for excavation)

### For Water-Based Exploration

- **Life jackets** (personal flotation devices)
- **Paddle or paddle/oars** (appropriate for watercraft)
- **Dry bags** (for protecting gear from water)
- **Water testing kit** (pH, dissolved oxygen, temperature)
- **Nets or collection containers**
- **Waders** (if entering water)
- **Waterproof camera or underwater camera**
- **Thermometer**

### For Cave Exploration

- **Multiple headlamps** (at least 2 per person)
- **Extra batteries**
- **Caving suit or protective clothing**
- **Helmet with headlamp mount**
- **Rope and rappelling equipment** (if entering deep caves)
- **First aid kit** (tailored for cave injuries)
- **Map of the cave**
- **Camera** (without flash, to avoid disturbing wildlife)

## Packing Strategy

**Organize by category:**
1. Items used daily (toiletries, medications, snacks)
2. Items used frequently (clothing, map, camera)
3. Items used occasionally (repair kit, first aid for serious injuries)
4. Emergency items (emergency shelter, signaling)

**Weight distribution:**
- Keep heaviest items close to your back (in backpack)
- Distribute weight evenly
- Keep frequently used items accessible

**Waterproofing:**
- Use dry bags or waterproof containers
- Keep first aid, map, and electronics dry
- Line backpack with a garbage bag

**Accessibility:**
- Keep lunch, water, and snacks on top or in side pockets
- Keep map and compass handy
- Keep headlamp easily accessible

{{< drg/safety-first >}}
Test all your gear BEFORE the expedition. Set up your tent in your yard. Wear your pack on a practice hike. Charge your batteries and GPS. Verify your stove works. Do not discover problems in the field.
{{< /drg/safety-first >}}

## Borrowing vs. Buying

You don't need to own everything:
- **Borrow** from your Scout troop, school, family, or friends
- **Rent** specialized equipment (climbing gear, kayak)
- **Borrow** from local outdoor clubs
- **Buy** only what you genuinely need and will use repeatedly
