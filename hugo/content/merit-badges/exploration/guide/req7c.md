Conduct a pre-expedition check, covering the steps in requirement 6, and share the results with your counselor. With your counselor, walk through the Scouting America SAFE Checklist for your expedition. Ensure that all foreseeable hazards for your expedition are adequately addressed.

Before you leave, you must verify that everything is actually ready. This is your final safety check.

## Pre-Expedition Checklist

Review all Requirement 6 areas one final time:

### Objectives ✓

- [ ] Objectives clearly stated and achievable
- [ ] Team understands the objectives
- [ ] Objectives are specific and measurable

### Mission Schedule ✓

- [ ] Detailed day-by-day schedule completed
- [ ] All permits obtained (copies in hand)
- [ ] Transportation confirmed (driver, dates, times)
- [ ] Accommodations confirmed (campground reserved, etc.)

### Budget ✓

- [ ] All expenses accounted for
- [ ] Funding secured
- [ ] Contingency fund available (10% extra)
- [ ] No unexpected costs

### Equipment & Supplies ✓

- [ ] All items on list obtained
- [ ] Equipment tested and functional
- [ ] Backpacks weight-tested
- [ ] Tent sets up correctly
- [ ] Stove works
- [ ] Headlamps work (batteries charged)
- [ ] Water filter works
- [ ] Repair kit complete
- [ ] Nothing left behind

### Communication & Transportation ✓

- [ ] Communication plan written and shared with family
- [ ] Check-in schedule confirmed
- [ ] Emergency contacts with all team members
- [ ] Team communication protocol clear
- [ ] Vehicle checked and ready
- [ ] All drivers have valid licenses
- [ ] Route planned and confirmed

### Safety & First Aid ✓

- [ ] All potential hazards identified
- [ ] Prevention procedures for each hazard
- [ ] First aid kit complete and checked
- [ ] Team knows CPR (or at least one member)
- [ ] Evacuation procedure clear
- [ ] Nearest medical facility identified
- [ ] Emergency numbers written down
- [ ] Medical information for all team members

### Team Selection & Roles ✓

- [ ] All team members confirmed and ready
- [ ] Roles clearly assigned
- [ ] Team has trained together
- [ ] Team has done a practice activity
- [ ] All members understand their responsibilities

### Documentation ✓

- [ ] Field notebooks prepared
- [ ] Camera(s) charged and tested
- [ ] Backup storage plan (cloud, external drive)
- [ ] Data recording templates prepared
- [ ] Specimen labels prepared
- [ ] Report template ready

## Scouting America SAFE Checklist

The SAFE Checklist (Activity Safety, Fair Play, Equipment, and Environment) is a comprehensive safety framework. Download the official checklist at scouting.org or review these key areas with your counselor:

### Activity Safety

- [ ] All participants meet physical requirements for the activity
- [ ] Appropriate supervision (counselor or qualified adult present)
- [ ] All participants trained on activity-specific skills
- [ ] No one participates under the influence of drugs/alcohol
- [ ] Clear objectives and rules for the activity
- [ ] Proper progression (not too advanced for skill level)
- [ ] Appropriate pace (not rushing)

### Fair Play & Inclusion

- [ ] All team members invited to participate
- [ ] No hazing or bullying
- [ ] Equal access to equipment
- [ ] Inclusive leadership (everyone's voice heard)

### Equipment & Facilities

- [ ] All equipment inspected and functional
- [ ] Equipment appropriate for the activity and conditions
- [ ] Safety equipment (life jackets, helmets, etc.) available and used
- [ ] Facilities checked for hazards
- [ ] Proper storage of equipment

### Environmental Considerations

- [ ] Leave No Trace principles followed
- [ ] Weather appropriate for the activity
- [ ] Sun protection available
- [ ] Insect protection available
- [ ] Water available and safe
- [ ] Waste disposal planned

## Hazard Assessment

Walk through your expedition systematically and identify *all* potential hazards:

### Terrain Hazards

- Steep slopes?
- Water crossings?
- Loose rock?
- Caves or tight spaces?
- Dense vegetation?

**Mitigation:** Know the terrain beforehand. Practice skills. Scout the route. Establish safety protocols.

### Weather Hazards

- Possible lightning?
- Extreme heat?
- Extreme cold?
- Flash flood risk?
- High wind?

**Mitigation:** Check forecast before departure. Bring extra layers. Know turnaround time if weather changes. Have shelter.

### Environmental Hazards

- Dangerous animals?
- Insects (ticks, mosquitoes)?
- Poisonous plants?
- Contaminated water?

**Mitigation:** Know what is present. Have repellent. Know how to identify and avoid. Have treatment supplies.

### Human Factor Hazards

- Inexperience with terrain?
- Physical fitness issues?
- Dehydration/heat illness?
- Fatigue?
- Separation from group?

**Mitigation:** Training. Physical conditioning. Adequate rest. Stay together. Set turnaround times.

### Equipment Hazards

- Equipment failure (tent, stove, rope)?
- Lost map or GPS?
- Headlamp battery dies?
- Communication failure?

**Mitigation:** Redundancy. Backups. Testing. Repair kits.

## Pre-Expedition Briefing

One week before departure, hold a final team briefing:

**Topics:**
1. Review objectives one final time
2. Go over the detailed schedule
3. Review safety procedures
4. Establish communication plan
5. Assign roles and responsibilities
6. Review equipment checklist
7. Address any final concerns
8. Get counselor approval

**Checklist:** All team members attend, understand their roles, and are ready to go.

---

## Final Approval

**Before departing, you must have:**

- [ ] Counselor approval of entire plan
- [ ] Counselor approval of safety procedures
- [ ] Counselor confirmation of supervision arrangement
- [ ] Parent/guardian permission
- [ ] All permits in hand
- [ ] All equipment packed
- [ ] Team briefing complete
- [ ] Communication plan in place with family

**Get this in writing** if possible. Have your counselor sign off on the plan.
