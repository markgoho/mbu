Use the steps listed in requirement 6 to guide your preparations. List the items of equipment and supplies you will need. Discuss with your counselor why you chose each item and how it will be of value on the expedition. Determine who should go on the expedition.

This requirement asks you to *actually execute* the detailed planning you did in Requirement 6. You should have written documents prepared, equipment assembled, and team members identified and trained.

## Translating Planning into Preparation

### Objectives (from Req 6a)

**Action items:**
- [ ] Write down your specific, measurable objectives
- [ ] Discuss with supervisor — are they achievable?
- [ ] Share with team — do they understand what you are trying to accomplish?

### Mission Schedule (from Req 6b)

**Action items:**
- [ ] Create detailed day-by-day schedule
- [ ] Identify permits needed
- [ ] [ Submit permit applications
- [ ] Obtain approved permits

**Timeline:**
- 12 weeks before: Permits identified
- 10 weeks before: Permits submitted
- 6 weeks before: Follow up on status
- 4 weeks before: Permits approved

### Budget (from Req 6c)

**Action items:**
- [ ] Complete detailed budget spreadsheet
- [ ] Identify funding sources
- [ ] Begin fundraising if needed
- [ ] Obtain cost estimates (campsite, travel, food)
- [ ] Track spending

**Timeline:**
- Start immediately; many costs need to be paid well in advance

### Equipment & Supplies (from Req 6d)

**Action items:**
- [ ] Create comprehensive equipment list
- [ ] Source each item (buy, borrow, rent)
- [ ] Test all equipment before expedition
- [ ] Pack and organize equipment
- [ ] Create backup/repair kit

**Checklist:**
```
PERSONAL GEAR:
- [ ] Tent (test set-up in yard)
- [ ] Sleeping bag (tested in home)
- [ ] Hiking boots (worn on practice hike)
- [ ] Backpack (weight-tested)
[... complete list ...]

MISSION EQUIPMENT:
- [ ] Field guide(s)
- [ ] Collection containers
- [ ] Measurement tools
[...]

SAFETY:
- [ ] First aid kit (assembled and checked)
- [ ] Headlamp (batteries tested)
[...]
```

### Communication & Transportation (from Req 6e)

**Action items:**
- [ ] Establish communication plan with family
- [ ] Create check-in schedule
- [ ] Arrange transportation
- [ ] Establish internal team communication protocol
- [ ] Provide emergency contacts to all team members

### Safety & First Aid (from Req 6f)

**Action items:**
- [ ] Identify all potential hazards
- [ ] Create prevention plan for each
- [ ] Assemble first aid kit
- [ ] Ensure team knows emergency procedures
- [ ] Identify evacuation route and procedures

### Team Selection & Roles (from Req 6g)

**Action items:**
- [ ] Identify all team members
- [ ] Assign roles (leader, specialist, support)
- [ ] Conduct training/skill-building
- [ ] Team practice (hike, climb, navigate together)
- [ ] Create team contact list

**Sample team meeting agenda:**
- 6 weeks before: First planning meeting, review objectives
- 4 weeks before: Skill training begins
- 2 weeks before: Final planning, safety briefing
- 1 week before: Equipment check-in, final preparations
- Day before: Final meeting, expectations review

### Documentation (from Req 6h)

**Action items:**
- [ ] Create field notebook template
- [ ] Assign documentation roles
- [ ] Test camera/video equipment
- [ ] Plan data storage and backup
- [ ] Prepare report template

## Discussing Equipment Choices with Your Counselor

Your counselor will want to understand *why* you chose each item. Be ready to explain:

**Example conversation:**

**Counselor:** "Why are you bringing two headlamps per person?"

**You:** "The primary headlamp might fail due to battery or bulb issues. A second headlamp ensures we always have light in the cave. Without light, we cannot navigate safely."

**Counselor:** "Why this specific tent?"

**You:** "It's a 3-person, 4-season tent. Three-person fits our team. Four-season means it handles rain, wind, and cold. We tested it before and we know it sets up correctly."

**Counselor:** "Why a GPS device when you have a map and compass?"

**You:** "GPS is our primary navigation tool. Map and compass are backups in case GPS batteries die or signal is lost. Having redundancy in navigation is critical."

**Counselor:** "Why are you bringing this water filter?"

**You:** "Our water sources might have parasites or bacteria. The filter removes them, preventing waterborne illness. We researched the water sources and chose a filter that removes the specific contaminants present."

## Team Determination

By this point, you should have:

1. **Identified all team members** — Who will go?
2. **Assigned roles** — Leader, specialists, support
3. **Verified skills** — Does each person have the skills they need?
4. **Conducted training** — Have team members learned necessary skills?
5. **Done a practice** — Did the team practice together successfully?

**Team selection checklist:**
- [ ] All members volunteer and are committed
- [ ] Physical fitness appropriate for expedition
- [ ] Necessary skills present or will be trained
- [ ] Team members work well together
- [ ] All members understand the objective
- [ ] All members understand safety procedures
- [ ] All members have permission from parents/guardians
