{{< drg/requirement number="7" >}}
Prepare for an Expedition. With your parent or guardian's permission and counselor's approval, prepare for an actual expedition to an area you have not previously explored; the place may be nearby or far away. Do the following:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

This requirement brings together everything you learned in Requirement 6. You are now applying all eight planning steps to your own real expedition. Here is what each sub-requirement asks of you:

- **Req 7a** — Work under the supervision of a qualified adult
- **Req 7b** — Use the Req 6 planning steps, list your equipment, justify each item, and select your team
- **Req 7c** — Conduct a pre-expedition check and walk through the SAFE Checklist

## 7a. Finding a Qualified Supervisor

{{< drg/requirement number="7a" >}}
Make your preparations under the supervision of a trained expedition leader, expedition planner, or other qualified adult experienced in exploration (such as a school science teacher, museum representative, or qualified instructor).
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Your expedition preparations need supervision from someone who has real experience with exploration or field research. This person does not have to be a professional explorer — they need practical knowledge of planning, safety, and working in the field.

Good candidates include:

- **Your merit badge counselor** — Many counselors have outdoor leadership or scientific fieldwork experience.
- **A science teacher** — Biology, ecology, geology, or environmental science teachers often have field research backgrounds.
- **A naturalist or park ranger** — Staff at nature centers, parks, and wildlife refuges plan and lead field expeditions regularly.
- **A museum professional** — Natural history museum staff frequently conduct fieldwork.
- **A Scout leader with relevant experience** — An assistant scoutmaster who is also a geologist, biologist, or environmental professional.

{{< drg/tip >}}
Ask your counselor for suggestions early. Finding the right supervisor takes time, and you want someone who can be genuinely involved in your planning — not just sign a form at the end.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

## 7b. Applying the Planning Steps

{{< drg/requirement number="7b" >}}
Use the steps listed in requirement 6 to guide your preparations. List the items of equipment and supplies you will need. Discuss with your counselor why you chose each item and how it will be of value on the expedition. Determine who should go on the expedition.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

This is where all your work from Requirement 6 comes together. Create a comprehensive expedition plan that addresses each step:

{{< drg/checklist title="Expedition Planning Recap" subtitle="Apply each Req 6 step to your actual expedition" >}}
- **Objectives (6a):** What is your primary question or goal? What are your secondary objectives?
- **Mission plan (6b):** What is your detailed schedule? What permits do you need?
- **Budget (6c):** What will the expedition cost? How will you fund it?
- **Equipment (6d):** What personal and mission gear do you need? Why?
- **Communication (6e):** How will you stay in contact with your base and with each other?
- **Safety (6f):** What hazards exist? What are your prevention and response procedures?
- **Team (6g):** Who is going? What role does each person play?
- **Documentation (6h):** How will you record and share your findings?
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

### Justifying Your Equipment

Your counselor will ask about every item on your equipment list. Be ready to connect each one to either personal safety or your expedition objectives. The framework from Req 6d applies here:

- "I am bringing a water quality test kit because my primary objective is to measure pH and dissolved oxygen levels at three stream sites."
- "I am bringing extra batteries because my GPS and camera are essential for navigation and documentation."
- "I am bringing a rain jacket because the forecast shows a 40% chance of afternoon showers and hypothermia is a risk if I get wet."

If you cannot justify an item, leave it behind. Extra weight slows you down and clutters your pack.

## 7c. The Pre-Expedition Check

{{< drg/requirement number="7c" >}}
Conduct a pre-expedition check, covering the steps in requirement 6, and share the results with your counselor. With your counselor, walk through the Scouting America SAFE Checklist for your expedition. Ensure that all foreseeable hazards for your expedition are adequately addressed.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

The pre-expedition check is your final review before launch. Think of it as a pilot's pre-flight checklist — no plane takes off without one, and no expedition should either.

### Running the Check

Go through each planning step one final time:

- **Objectives:** Still clear and achievable? Has anything changed?
- **Schedule:** Realistic? Does it account for travel time, breaks, and contingencies?
- **Permits:** In hand? Printed and packed?
- **Budget:** Funded? All purchases made?
- **Equipment:** Assembled? Tested? Batteries charged? Nothing forgotten?
- **Communication:** Base contact briefed? Radios tested? Check-in times confirmed?
- **Safety:** First-aid kit stocked? Evacuation route known? Hazards identified and planned for?
- **Team:** Everyone confirmed? Roles assigned? Everyone briefed on the plan?
- **Documentation:** Notebooks ready? Data sheets printed? Camera charged?

### The SAFE Checklist

Walk through the Scouting America SAFE Checklist with your counselor:

- **Supervised** — A qualified adult will supervise all activities
- **Age-appropriate** — All activities are suitable for the ages and abilities of the participants
- **Fitness** — All participants are physically and mentally prepared for the planned activities
- **Equipment and Environment** — The right gear is available and the environment has been assessed for hazards

{{< drg/safety-first >}}
The pre-expedition check is not a formality — it is your last chance to catch a problem before you are in the field. If something does not check out, fix it or postpone the expedition. No data is worth a safety compromise.
{{< /drg/safety-first >}}
