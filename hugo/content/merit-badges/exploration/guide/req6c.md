{{< drg/requirement number="6c" >}}
Budget and plan for adequate financial resources. Estimate costs for travel, equipment, accommodations, meals, permits or licenses, and other expedition expenses.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Exploration costs money. From transportation to equipment to meals, every expedition has a budget. This requirement asks you to estimate all costs and plan how to cover them.

## Budget Categories

### Travel & Transportation

- **Vehicle gas or fuel:** If driving, calculate round-trip mileage × fuel cost
- **Public transportation:** Bus, train, or plane tickets
- **Ground transportation at destination:** Shuttles, vehicle rental, local transport
- **Parking fees**

**Example:** Driving 200 miles round trip at $0.50/mile = $100

### Accommodations

- **Campsite fees:** Many campgrounds charge $20–$50/night
- **Cabin rental:** $50–$200/night
- **Hotel:** $80–$200/night
- **Permits for camping on public land:** $10–$50

**Example:** 3 nights at $30/night = $90

### Food & Water

- **Meals for the team** (breakfast, lunch, dinner)
- **Snacks and energy food**
- **Drinking water** (if not available at site)
- **Fuel for camp cooking**

**Example:** 5 people × 3 days × $15/person/day = $225

### Equipment & Gear

- **Specialized field equipment:** Nets, collection containers, measurement devices
- **Camping gear:** Tent, sleeping bags, stove (if not already owned)
- **Safety equipment:** First aid kit, emergency signaling, headlamps
- **Field notebook, camera, GPS device**

**Example:** Binoculars $80, field guide books $40, collection containers $30 = $150

### Permits & Licenses

- **Research permits:** $0–$500
- **Parking permits**
- **Special use permits**

**Example:** Research permit = $50

### Miscellaneous

- **Insurance or emergency fund**
- **Contingency for unexpected costs** (repairs, emergency supplies)
- **Communication** (phone, satellite messenger)

## Sample Budget

**Ecology Expedition (5 people, 3 days, local)**

| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Vehicle fuel (200 miles @ $0.50/mile) | $100 |
| Campground fee (3 nights @ $30) | $90 |
| Food ($15/person/day × 5 × 3) | $225 |
| Permits | $50 |
| Field equipment (nets, containers, guidebooks) | $120 |
| First aid supplies | $30 |
| Gas for camp stove | $20 |
| Contingency (10%) | $63 |
| **TOTAL** | **$698** |

## Funding Your Expedition

Once you know the costs, how do you pay for them?

### Option 1: Scout/Family Resources

- Allowance or savings
- Family contribution
- Troop or patrol budget
- Grant from your school

### Option 2: Fundraising

- Car washes
- Bake sales
- Yard work for community members
- Ask local businesses for sponsorships
- Crowdfunding (with family permission)

### Option 3: Grants & Sponsorships

Some organizations provide small grants for youth exploration projects:
- Your local parks department
- Community foundations
- Scout councils
- Local outdoor clubs
- Environmental organizations

### Option 4: In-Kind Donations

Ask local businesses to donate equipment or services:
- Outdoor store donates field gear
- Restaurant donates food
- Gas station donates fuel cards
- Hotel donates a cabin at discount

{{< drg/tip >}}
Be specific when asking for donations. Instead of "Can you help with our expedition?" say "We need $50 for permits and $30 for field guides. Could your business sponsor these items?"
{{< /drg/tip >}}

## Cost-Saving Strategies

- **Use existing equipment** you already own (tent, backpack, GPS)
- **Camp instead of hotel** (much cheaper)
- **Cook your own meals** instead of eating out
- **Combine resources** with other Scouts
- **Choose a local site** to minimize travel costs
- **Apply for grants** from your Scout council
- **Ask counselor/adults** about equipment you can borrow
- **Time your expedition** during off-season when permits are cheaper

---

## Document Your Budget

When you meet with your counselor, be prepared to show:

1. **Detailed budget spreadsheet** with all categories and costs
2. **How you arrived at each estimate** (quotes from vendors, online research, etc.)
3. **Funding source** — Where will the money come from?
4. **Contingency plan** — What if costs are higher than expected?
