{{< drg/requirement number="9" >}}
Career Opportunities: Do ONE of the following:

a. Explore careers related to this merit badge. Research one career to learn about the training and education needed, costs, job prospects, salary, job duties, and career advancement. Your research methods may include—with your parent or guardian's permission—an internet or library search, an interview with a professional in the field, or a visit to a location where people in this career work. Discuss with your counselor both your findings and what about this profession might make it an interesting career.

b. Explore how you could use knowledge and skills from this merit badge to pursue a hobby. Research any training needed, expenses, and organizations that promote or support it. Discuss with your counselor what short-term and long-term goals you might have if you pursued this.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

The Exploration merit badge opens many doors — both career doors and hobby doors. This final requirement asks you to look ahead and imagine how exploration could be part of your future.

## Option Overview

### Option A: Exploration Career Research

Choose a career related to exploration — scientist, guide, researcher, explorer, conservation officer, etc. Research it in depth. Learn about education required, salary, job duties, and whether it interests you as a potential career path.

[Continue to Option A](req9a)

### Option B: Exploration Hobby

Think about how you could continue exploring as a hobby throughout your life. What kind of exploration would you enjoy doing regularly? What skills would you need? What would it cost? What organizations support this hobby?

[Continue to Option B](req9b)

## Exploration Careers

If you choose Option A, here are some exploration-related careers to consider:

### Scientific Research

- **Field Biologist:** Research animals and ecosystems in natural habitats
- **Paleontologist:** Study ancient life through fossils
- **Geologist:** Investigate Earth's structure and mineral resources
- **Marine Biologist:** Study ocean organisms and ecosystems
- **Astronomer:** Observe and study distant celestial objects
- **Environmental Scientist:** Monitor and protect natural environments

### Adventure & Outdoor

- **Mountain Guide:** Lead expeditions on mountains and peaks
- **Expedition Leader:** Plan and lead exploration missions
- **Outdoor Educator:** Teach wilderness skills and environmental science
- **Conservation Officer:** Protect natural areas and wildlife
- **Park Ranger:** Manage public lands and educate visitors

### Museum & Cultural

- **Archaeologist:** Excavate and study ancient human settlements
- **Museum Curator:** Preserve and interpret artifacts and specimens
- **Anthropologist:** Study human cultures and societies
- **Conservation Specialist:** Preserve cultural artifacts

### Technology & Support

- **Geographic Information Systems (GIS) Specialist:** Map and analyze geographic data
- **Data Scientist:** Analyze exploration data
- **Equipment Engineer:** Design equipment for explorers
- **Science Journalist:** Report on exploration discoveries

## Exploration Hobbies

If you choose Option B, think about ways you could continue exploring:

### Hobby Explorations

- **Birdwatching:** Identify and track local bird species
- **Geology/Rockhounding:** Collect and study rocks and minerals
- **Citizen Science:** Contribute to scientific research projects online or locally
- **Hiking & Backpacking:** Explore new trails and wilderness areas
- **Kayaking/Canoeing:** Explore local waterways
- **Cave Exploration (caving):** Explore cave systems
- **Scuba Diving:** Explore underwater ecosystems
- **Wildlife Photography:** Document animals and habitats
- **Archaeology Volunteer:** Participate in archaeological digs
- **Local History:** Explore historical sites in your community
