{{< drg/requirement number="8b2" >}}
Oceanographic institute, marine laboratory, or marine aquarium
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

This option lets you see ocean science in a place built for research, teaching, conservation, or animal care. A marine lab and an aquarium are not exactly the same, but both can reveal how people study and explain the ocean.

## What to Watch For

At a marine lab or institute, notice:

- research equipment
- sample storage or wet lab spaces
- maps, sensors, and data displays
- what kinds of questions scientists are studying

At an aquarium, notice:

- how exhibits explain habitats and food webs
- how animal care depends on water quality and observation
- how the aquarium teaches the public about conservation and ocean science

## Questions to Ask

- What kinds of projects happen here?
- How do scientists collect samples or data?
- What careers are represented in this place?
- How does this organization help people understand or protect the ocean?

{{< drg/tip >}}
If the visit includes signs or exhibit panels, photograph or jot down specific facts that surprised you. Specific details make your 500-word report much stronger.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

The final option in Req 8 changes from writing about a visit to giving a short speech on oceanography's value or career paths.
