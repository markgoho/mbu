{{< drg/requirement number="8" >}}
Do TWO of the following and discuss with your counselor:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

You must choose exactly 2 options from this requirement. This page helps you compare the choices so you can pick the two that fit your interests, opportunities, and long-term goals.

## Your Options

- **[Req 8a — Keep Participating](/merit-badges/multisport/guide/req8a/)**: Find two realistic ways to stay involved in multisport after the badge.
- **[Req 8b — Study a Triathlete](/merit-badges/multisport/guide/req8b/)**: Research an athlete and learn what their story can teach you.
- **[Req 8c — Lead a Training Group](/merit-badges/multisport/guide/req8c/)**: Turn what you learned into leadership by organizing others.
- **[Req 8d — Enter a Sanctioned Event](/merit-badges/multisport/guide/req8d/)**: Step into a real organized event in your area.
- **[Req 8e — Volunteer at an Event](/merit-badges/multisport/guide/req8e/)**: Support athletes and organizers by helping behind the scenes.

## How to Choose

{{< drg/checklist title="Choosing your final two options" subtitle="Think about access, time, and what you want to gain" >}}
- **If you want to stay active yourself:** 8a and 8d are strong choices.
- **If you want to learn from role models and ideas:** 8a and 8b fit well.
- **If you want leadership experience:** 8c and 8e give you chances to help others.
- **If local events are hard to access:** 8a, 8b, and 8c may be more realistic than 8d.
- **What you will gain:** Some choices build participation, some build inspiration, and some build service and leadership.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

{{< drg/tip >}}
Pick options you can complete thoughtfully, not just quickly. A meaningful discussion with your counselor will be easier if your choices fit real opportunities in your area.
{{< /drg/tip >}}

The first option looks at realistic ways to keep multisport part of your life after the badge ends.
