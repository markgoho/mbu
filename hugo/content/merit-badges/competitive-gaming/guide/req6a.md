{{< drg/requirement number="6a" option="Option A — Teaching Safety" >}}
Teach your troop, patrol, or a group of younger Scouts or family members about online gaming safety or digital citizenship.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Option A gives you an opportunity to exercise Scout leadership by teaching others how to stay safe and courteous in online spaces.

## Lesson Planning Outline

To deliver an engaging 15-to-20 minute presentation:

1. **Opening Hook (3 mins)**: Share a quick story or question about online gaming habits to get your audience engaged.
2. **Core Presentation (10 mins)**: Cover three key areas:
   - **Privacy Protection**: Keeping personal data, real names, and locations private.
   - **Handling Toxicity**: Using mute, block, and report tools instead of arguing.
   - **The Scout Law Online**: How being friendly, courteous, and kind applies to gaming lobbies.
3. **Interactive Demonstration (5 mins)**: Show your audience where to find privacy settings on a console or gaming PC.
4. **Q&A and Wrap-up (2 mins)**: Answer questions and distribute a quick tip checklist.

{{< drg/tip >}}
Use visual aids, slide decks, or printed tip sheets to help your patrol remember key safety rules!
{{< /drg/tip >}}
