## Requirement 7a: Collegiate Varsity Esports Programs

{{< drg/requirement number="7a" >}}
Research two colleges that offer varsity esports programs. Record the following information for each: availability and amount of gaming scholarships, offered gaming or IT-related degrees, games played by the esports team, league or association in which the team competes.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Over 200 universities in North America offer varsity esports programs with dedicated gaming arenas, athletic department sponsorship, and tuition scholarships.

### Research Framework for Two Universities

When conducting your college research, document:

1. **University Name & Location**: (e.g., *Boise State University* or *University of California, Irvine*).
2. **Esports Scholarships**: Financial aid amounts offered to roster athletes.
3. **Academic Degrees**: Degree programs related to gaming (e.g., *B.S. in Computer Science*, *Game Design*, *Interactive Media*, or *Information Technology*).
4. **Varsity Titles Played**: Specific competitive titles fielded by the university (e.g., *Rocket League*, *Overwatch 2*, *Valorant*).
5. **Collegiate League Affiliation**: Governing bodies (e.g., *NACE - National Association of Collegiate Esports*, *NECC*, or *PlayVS College*).

## Requirement 7b: Gaming-Adjacent Careers

{{< drg/requirement number="7b" >}}
Research three gaming-adjacent careers that support or connect to the esports industry but are not esports athlete roles. Examples may include: game designer or developer, shoutcaster or broadcast producer, data analyst or statistician, marketing or event coordinator, IT or network support specialist. For each career, identify the main responsibilities, education or training requirements, and how the role contributes to the success of the gaming or esports ecosystem.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Choose three non-athlete roles that power the gaming ecosystem:

### 1. Broadcast Producer / Technical Director
- **Responsibilities**: Manages live multi-camera video feeds, audio mixing, graphics overlays, and commercial timing during live esports broadcasts.
- **Education & Skills**: Degree in Film, Broadcasting, Communications, or Live Production; proficiency with OBS, vMix, and switcher consoles.
- **Contribution**: Delivers polished, professional tournament broadcasts to global streaming audiences.

### 2. Esports Data Analyst / Statistician
- **Responsibilities**: Analyzes match telemetry data, win/loss metrics, item pick-rates, and player positioning to support coaching strategies or broadcast graphics.
- **Education & Skills**: Degree in Data Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or Computer Science; SQL, Python, and data visualization tools.
- **Contribution**: Gives teams a competitive tactical edge and provides broadcast audiences with deep statistical insights.

### 3. Game Designer & Developer
- **Responsibilities**: Writes code, designs competitive map layouts, balances character abilities, and implements server netcode.
- **Education & Skills**: Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or Game Design; C++, C#, Unity, or Unreal Engine.
- **Contribution**: Creates the underlying games, mechanics, and fair competitive systems that make esports possible.

## Requirement 7c: Major Game Publisher Career Pages

{{< drg/requirement number="7c" >}}
Visit the "Careers" or "Jobs" page of a major game publisher (such as Blizzard, Riot, Epic or Nintendo). Choose three jobs that interest you. For each, identify the required education, skills, and experience.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Visit the official career portals of game publishers and select three real job postings:

- **Blizzard Entertainment**: Focuses on global franchises like *World of Warcraft* and *Overwatch*. Look for roles in game engineering, art, and community operations.
- **Riot Games**: Developer of *League of Legends* and *Valorant*. Features openings in network infrastructure, anti-cheat technology, and esports production.
- **Epic Games**: Creator of Unreal Engine and *Fortnite*. Highlights careers in graphics programming, engine tools, and online services.
- **Nintendo of America**: Industry pioneer focused on system architecture, game localization, quality assurance, and event logistics.

For each job posting you select, write down:
1. Job Title & Department
2. Required Education (Degree level)
3. Essential Technical & Soft Skills
4. Years of Professional Experience required
