## Requirement 4a: Benefits and Risks of Online Gaming

{{< drg/requirement number="4a" >}}
Explain the benefits and risks of gaming online.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

### Online Gaming Balance Sheet

- **Benefits**:
  - **Global Cooperation**: Working alongside players across different regions builds cultural awareness and cross-team communication.
  - **Problem-Solving under Pressure**: Fast-paced multiplayer environments develop quick decision-making and tactical adaptability.
  - **Shared Community**: Joining school leagues or patrol gaming groups strengthens social connections around shared interests.

- **Risks**:
  - **Privacy Exposure**: Sharing personal information (real name, location, school, age) with online strangers.
  - **Cyberbullying & Harassment**: Encountering hostile players in unmonitored voice or text lobbies.
  - **Financial Risks**: Accidental microtransactions, loot boxes, or phishing scams targeting account credentials.

## Requirement 4b: Ten Rules for Online Gaming Safety

{{< drg/requirement number="4b" >}}
Research and list ten ways to stay safe when playing video games online.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

{{< drg/checklist title="10 Online Gaming Safety Rules" subtitle="Essential rules for every connected gamer" >}}
- **1. Keep Personal Information Private**: Never share your real name, address, school, phone number, or social media handles.
- **2. Use a Neutral Gamertag**: Create a username that does not reveal your real name, age, birth year, or location.
- **3. Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**: Protect your gaming accounts (Steam, Xbox, PlayStation, Epic) with authenticator apps or SMS verification.
- **4. Never Share Passwords**: Keep passwords secret even from close friends.
- **5. Use Platform Privacy Controls**: Set your profile, friend list, and online status to "Friends Only" or "Private."
- **6. Mute and Block Hostile Players**: Utilize built-in mute and block features at the first sign of abusive behavior.
- **7. Beware of Phishing Scams**: Never click external links claiming to offer "free in-game currency," skins, or hacks.
- **8. Report Terms-of-Service Violations**: Use official reporting tools to document hate speech, cheating, or threats.
- **9. Keep Gaming Audio in Common Living Areas**: Avoid using headsets in private bedrooms during unmonitored multiplayer sessions.
- **10. Talk to a Trusted Adult**: Immediately inform a parent, guardian, or leader if an online interaction makes you feel uncomfortable.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

## Requirement 4c: 30-Minute Parent Co-Play Session

{{< drg/requirement number="4c" >}}
Play one of your favorite games with your parent or guardian for at least 30 minutes. Demonstrate how to use privacy and safety settings, explain what you enjoy about the game, and let them play for part of the session.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

### Co-Play Session Outline

1. **Orientation (5 mins)**: Show your parent or guardian the main menu, game objective, and control layout. Explain why you enjoy the game.
2. **Safety Settings Tour (5 mins)**: Open the game's system menu. Demonstrate how to adjust voice chat volume, set matchmaking privacy, block players, and check parental control options.
3. **Hands-On Co-Play (15 mins)**: Pass the controller or keyboard to your parent! Guide them through a beginner level or practice tutorial, offering encouraging coaching.
4. **Debrief (5 mins)**: Discuss what they noticed about the game's mechanics, speed, and safety features.

## Requirement 4d: Team Multiplayer Match & Communication Review

{{< drg/requirement number="4d" >}}
Play an online or local multiplayer game with another Scout, friend, or community member in a cooperative or team-based setting. The match may be casual or competitive. During play, demonstrate positive communication, teamwork, and sportsmanship. Afterward, review the experience with your counselor and describe how cooperation and communication influenced your team's performance and overall enjoyment of the game.
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

### Key Communication Habits to Practice

- **Clear Callouts**: Share short, objective information (e.g., *"Item available on the right,"* *"Fall back to defend"*).
- **Active Listening**: Pay attention to your teammates' calls without talking over them.
- **Positive Reinforcement**: Acknowledge good plays and maintain team morale even when behind in points.

After the session, prepare to discuss with your counselor how clear communication directly affected your team's performance and match enjoyment.
