Option B focuses on analyzing digital broadcasting and tournament production.

## Observation Guide for Streamed Events

While tuning into a live-streamed esports match (on Twitch, YouTube Gaming, or a school league stream), take notes on:

1. **Broadcast Presentation & Production**:
   - How do shoutcasters explain tactical setups and play-by-play actions?
   - How do overlay graphics display real-time player statistics, scoreboards, and mini-maps?

{{< drg/image src="images/esports-broadcast-overlay.avif" alt="Diagram illustrating an esports stream layout showing match camera view, minimap, player cams, score header, and shoutcaster window" >}}
2. **Player & Team Communication**:
   - How do team webcams, audio snippets, or post-match interviews reflect player focus and team coordination?
3. **Digital Audience Interaction**:
   - How does the online chat interact with stream milestones, emotes, and viewer polls?
