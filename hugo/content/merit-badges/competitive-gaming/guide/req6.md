{{< drg/requirement number="6" >}}
Leadership and Service in Competitive Gaming. Do ONE of the following:
{{< /drg/requirement >}}

Requirement 6 asks you to apply your gaming knowledge through leadership, event analysis, or live community participation. You must complete **exactly ONE** of the following options.

## Option Summaries

- **[Req 6a — Teaching Digital Citizenship](/merit-badges/competitive-gaming/guide/req6a/)**: Prepare and present a lesson on online gaming safety or digital citizenship to your patrol, troop, or family members.
- **[Req 6b — Streamed Esports Event Analysis](/merit-badges/competitive-gaming/guide/req6b/)**: Watch a live-streamed collegiate, professional, or school esports broadcast online and take structured notes on production, shoutcasting, and player communication.
- **[Req 6c — Live Tournament Experience](/merit-badges/competitive-gaming/guide/req6c/)**: Attend an in-person esports tournament, convention, or gaming competition with another Scout or family member and observe the venue atmosphere.

## How to Choose

{{< drg/checklist title="Choosing Your Leadership Path" subtitle="Compare your options before deciding" >}}
- **Req 6a (Teaching & Service)**: Best if you enjoy public speaking, teaching younger Scouts, and putting Scout leadership directly into practice. *What you'll gain*: Public speaking confidence and teaching experience.
- **Req 6b (Online Broadcast Analysis)**: Best if you enjoy broadcast media, shoutcasting, game analysis, and watching live tournament streams online from home. *What you'll gain*: Insight into esports production, commentary, and digital spectatorship.
- **Req 6c (Live Event Participation)**: Best if you can attend a local LAN tournament, gaming convention, or high school/college esports event in person. *What you'll gain*: Firsthand understanding of live arena energy, stage presentation, and community atmosphere.
{{< /drg/checklist >}}

{{< drg/tip >}}
If you are currently serving as your troop's Webmaster or Bugler, or preparing a Cyber Chip / Digital Safety presentation, Option 6a fulfills a great service opportunity for your troop!
{{< /drg/tip >}}
